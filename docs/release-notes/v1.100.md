## 1.100.0 — 2026-10-19

This completes the itemized sweep that [v1.99](v1.99.md) prepared for. Sweeping online and event money to the
Foundation no longer means typing one lump amount: each gift is copied onto the Foundation's books as its own
entry, and the one real bank transfer is checked against the gifts it is made of. **The old single-amount sweep is
retired.**

### Feature: Itemized sweep to the Foundation

**Value:** The Foundation issues the receipts, but until now its books saw only one anonymous lump. Now the
Foundation's register, donor history, Form 990 preparation and acknowledgments list all see the real gifts, each
under its own donor and on the date it was given, while the Club's books show one transfer out. The total is checked
against the amount that actually reached the bank, so a sweep that does not add up cannot be posted.

#### What's New

- **A sweep builder.** On the Public Funds register, **Sweep to the Foundation** opens `/admin/ledger/sweep`. It lists
  the gifts in Public Funds that have not been swept, whose category has a Foundation counterpart. Tick the gifts to
  sweep, type the amount that actually reached the bank, and review. **Post stays off until the typed amount equals
  the total of the ticked gifts**, and under the field the page says in words how far apart they are. There is no
  tolerance and nothing is rounded or plugged to make it match. A batch holds up to 200 gifts, needs a board-minute
  reference, and its date cannot be in the future.
- **Post, or send to the board.** A sweep at or under the Ledger's approval threshold (set at $1,000 today) posts at
  once. A sweep over it waits as one decision for a Board member with Approve Ledger, who is emailed a count and a
  total with no names or memos; it posts when one approver agrees, and the person who recorded it cannot decide it.
  **When a sweep goes to the board, "Posting…" can take up to half a minute while the approvers are emailed.** Wait
  for it; pressing again does not create a second batch. If the page loses the answer, it says so and shows the
  real state instead of announcing a failure.
- **What a batch does to each book.**
  - *On the Foundation's register,* each gift appears as its own income entry under the Foundation category that its
    Public Funds category points to, with the gift's original date, donor, cause, memo and payment method. The check
    number, receipt file, public note and budget line are not carried over.
  - *On the Public Funds register,* the batch is one transfer entry for the total, in a category called **Transfer to
    Foundation**. The original gifts stay as Public Funds income and are marked swept.
  - *Locks.* A swept gift, its copy and the transfer entry cannot be edited, split, moved or deleted. On a copy the
    donor can still be linked or changed. Each row says what swept it, and a chip on the transfer entry opens the
    batch.
  - *Totals.* The batch adds exactly its total to the Foundation's gross receipts and Form 990 preparation and
    changes nothing in the Club's totals or its 990 preparation, because the transfer entry is a transfer and the
    copies are the Foundation's own income.
- **Receipts follow the Foundation copy, and you send them.** A copy of $250 or more in a category that needs a
  receipt joins the **Acknowledgments** list on the Foundation's books, and the letter is emailed from the portal.
  Zeffy's own email does not replace the club's letter. A gift whose receipt was already sent is not queued again and
  cannot be acknowledged a second time. Sweep transfer entries no longer appear in the Acknowledgments list. As
  everywhere in the Ledger, the screen says "Emailed", never "Delivered".
- **Approvals shows a batch as one decision.** A sweep waiting for the board appears as a single row with a summary:
  how many gifts, the total, the span of gift dates, and the totals by Foundation category. The approver sees counts
  and categories, not donors or memos. Approving posts every copy together; rejecting rejects them together, and the
  gifts become sweepable again. A rejected batch's entries stay visible on the Foundation's register, marked rejected.
- **Void.** Until something closes the batch, a **Manage Ledger** holder can void a posted batch with a written
  reason, which removes its copies and frees its gifts. A batch can no longer be voided once a bank statement has
  cleared it, it has been matched in reconciliation, a receipt has been sent for one of its gifts, or the board has
  approved it. A pending batch is rejected from Approvals instead. A batch from an earlier fiscal year, or one that
  holds a gift in a year the Foundation's IRS filing is marked filed, cannot be voided either. Each refusal says why
  and what to do next. A batch the board approved has no undo in the app: record a correcting entry on each book.
- **"Mark as already handled."** For a gift that an earlier sweep already carried, a Manage Ledger holder can mark it
  handled with a reason, which takes it off the sweep list so it is never swept twice. It is reversible from the same
  page and recorded in the audit log.
- **Match the transfer to the bank in one step.** In reconciliation, the Foundation's single bank credit shows the
  batch's gifts as one group, "Sweep of N gifts", and **Match this batch** ticks exactly that group. A group that does
  not add up to the bank line is refused as always.
- **The single-amount sweep is retired.** Recording a Transfer from Public Funds to the Foundation as one amount is
  refused with a message pointing to the builder. A sweep recorded the old way before this release can still be
  approved, rejected and co-signed; **Record sweep now** in the Move window opens the builder with the moved gift
  already ticked. Transfers within one fund
  are unchanged.
- **Prior fiscal years.** A batch may include gifts from an earlier fiscal year, and the copies keep their original
  dates. That needs **Manage Ledger** and a typed reason, and the review warns when a month's statement was already
  sent, because that month will read differently. It is refused when the Foundation's IRS filing for that year is
  marked filed. The app only knows the years its filing tracker lists, so a year with no entry reads as not filed;
  confirm the year outside the app first.
- **The donor page** shows a swept gift once with its copy labelled, and Total Giving counts it once.

#### First production batch: what the treasurer should do

1. **Key the gifts first.** Nothing imports from Zeffy yet. The sweep list shows only gifts recorded in Public
   Funds under a category that has a Foundation counterpart. Record each gift with its donor, otherwise the
   Foundation's donor history will not show it. If you have only a total, record it as one gift (Anonymous is fine)
   and sweep that; there is no other way to sweep an amount now.
2. **Check each category you will use.** **Zeffy Donations**, **Public donations** and **White Cane** are ready. For
   Rudolph Run, Pancake Breakfast, Sponsorships and Interest, either set a Foundation counterpart (Settings,
   Categories) or leave them: money in a category with no counterpart stays in Public Funds and sits under "Held
   back" by design. Reactivate an inactive category only if you will key gifts into it. **Never point a counterpart at
   the Foundation's Transfer from Club** (it is marked "no receipt needed", so those gifts would never be queued for
   a receipt), and leave Transfer to Foundation inactive; the sweep uses it by itself.
3. **Set the Foundation's White Cane Form 990 line before sweeping any White Cane gift.** The campaign runs to
   October 31. Without a line, the copies report as "Unmapped / White Cane" in the Foundation's 990 preparation.
4. **Decide the three 2025 gifts before the first batch.** Two are Public donations; one is Pancake Breakfast,
   which has no counterpart and will be held back. If the October 1 sweep already carried a gift, mark it as already
   handled so it is never swept twice. Anything you do sweep from 2025 is a prior-year batch: it needs Manage Ledger
   and a reason, and the copies carry their January to March 2025 dates on the Foundation's books. Confirm the
   Foundation's FY2025 filing status yourself first, and read any "a monthly statement was already sent" notice in
   the review. The simplest first batch is a current-year gift; the 2025 gifts can wait.
5. **At the review,** type the amount that actually moved at the bank. A board-minute reference is required on
   every sweep. At or under $1,000 it posts at once; over $1,000 it goes to a Board member with Approve Ledger (the
   treasurer is not an approver) and posts when one agrees. If it goes to the board, allow up to half a minute for
   "Posting…". Note how long the first one takes.
6. **Know the undo window.** A sweep that posted at once can be voided by a Manage Ledger holder until a bank
   statement clears it, it is matched in reconciliation, or a receipt is sent for one of its gifts. Once the board
   approves a batch there is no undo in the app.
7. **After posting, send the receipts from the portal.** On the Foundation's books, each copy of $250 or more in a
   category that needs a receipt appears in Acknowledgments.
8. **At the bank.** When the transfer clears, match the Club's debit to the batch's transfer entry, and in the
   Foundation's reconciliation use **Match this batch** on the single credit line.

#### Also open for the treasurer

These are the decisions carried over from [v1.99](v1.99.md) that the sweep now depends on:

- **Reactivate the Public Funds categories you use.** Most are still inactive from the August clean-up, and the gift
  form and the sweep list only use active ones.
- **Pick a counterpart, or leave it as "stays in Public Funds,"** for each category you reactivate (see step 2).
- **Give the Foundation's White Cane category a Form 990 line** (step 3).

#### Known rough edges

- The sweep list's **Held back** group mixes gifts that need attention with gifts that stay in Public Funds by
  design, and it reads only the oldest 500 matching entries. Both are small and tracked.
- The donor page counts a pending batch's copies in Total Giving, and a rejected batch's copies stay in the donor's
  history and total. This is display only: receipts and every report already ignore entries that are not posted.
- The memo on a swept copy can be changed through the system but has no control on screen yet; only the donor link
  does.

#### Permissions

- None new. **Record Ledger** builds and posts a sweep; **Manage Ledger** voids a batch, marks a gift as already
  handled, and is required for a prior-year batch; **Approve Ledger** decides a batch over the threshold.
