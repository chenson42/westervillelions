## 1.98.0 — 2026-10-13

### Feature: Santa's Workshop family page and retention

**Value:** Every stuffy now has its own photo page. A family scans the code on the volunteer's phone at pickup and sees its stuffy's portrait right away, can save it and print it, and gets a link to the whole day's photos once the club has published them. After the event the club can take every photo down and delete the sign-up details from its records from one screen, in steps it can pause and resume; the printed codes keep working and then say the photos have been removed.

#### What's New

- The family page prints as a keepsake sheet with the portrait, the stuffy's name and a code to the day's photos. It never shows a name other than the stuffy's.
- Admins have a Retention card on each session: it shows when sign-up details and photos are due to come down, and two buttons do it, each with a confirmation that says what is deleted and that it cannot be undone. Taking the photos down removes them everywhere at once, including the family pages and the collage. Running either step early requires a written reason.
- A practice session can be reset to run the drill again; a real session never can.
- The registrants list can filter to stuffies checked in without a photo, and closing a session warns if phones still hold photos that have not sent.

#### Permissions

- None new. Retention actions use the existing Santa's Workshop permission.

#### New Routes

- `GET /santas-workshop/f/<token>` — a family's page (reached from the pickup QR)
