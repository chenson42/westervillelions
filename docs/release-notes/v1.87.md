## 1.87.0 — 2026-10-01

This release changes one thing the v1.86 notes told you to do by hand. v1.86 said that a gift recorded on
the Foundation's books but actually deposited in the Club's bank account was "not a move" and had to be
deleted and entered again. That case is now a move. The opposite case (a Club gift whose cash landed in
the Foundation's account) is still delete and re-enter.

### Feature: Move a gift from the Foundation's books to the Club's

**Value:** When a gift was recorded on the Foundation's books but the money was actually deposited in the
Club's bank account, the treasurer can now move the entry across in one step. The donor and the receipt
that was already sent stay with the gift, and nothing has to be deleted and typed in again.

#### What's New

- **Move to the Club.** On a Foundation income entry, choose **Move**, pick the Club's Activity Fund, pick
  the Club bank account the money landed in, choose a category (Public donations is preselected), and give
  a reason. The bank account is never guessed for you unless the Club has only one active account. Before
  you confirm, the dialog shows the before and after for both funds and both bank accounts, because
  unlike a move inside the Club, the bank balances really do change.
- **The donor and the receipt stay.** The gift keeps its donor, and a receipt letter that was already sent
  stays attached to it and still names the Foundation as the issuer. The Club's register marks it
  "Receipt on file, issued by the Foundation."
- **A receipt that was prepared but never sent is removed.** A Club entry cannot hold one, so an unsent
  receipt draft on the moved gift is removed with the move. The dialog says so first, and nothing was
  sent to the donor.
- **A step-by-step checklist for reconciled gifts.** If a closed reconciliation session owns the entry,
  the dialog does not move anything. It shows a checklist instead: reopen the session (the newest
  later session first, if there are later closed ones), unmatch the gift, move it, give the freed bank
  line its correct entry, close the session again, then record the sweep. Nothing on the checklist
  happens automatically, and it links straight to the session. The same checklist appears if the entry
  is matched to a bank line in a session that is still open.
- **Then sweep.** The success screen offers **Record sweep now**, which opens the Sweep to Foundation form
  for the Activity Fund with the amount, the date of the move and the bank account filled in, and a memo
  saying the gift was moved from the Foundation. The board-minute reference is still yours to enter.
  Moving a gift does not sweep anything by itself.
- **A warning if the deposit may already be recorded** on the Club's books, so the same money is not
  counted twice. It looks for Club income of the same amount within 30 days, so a deposit
  that was bundled with other money cannot be detected.
- **The move is on both Compliance pages.** The Foundation's and the Club's Compliance pages list it under
  **Recent corrections** as "Foundation to Club", with both bank accounts, the reason, and a "Receipt
  already sent" badge when one had been sent.

#### What the treasurer should know

- **Who can do it, and what is required.** Moving a gift to the Club needs the Manage Ledger permission
  (held by administrators by default), a reason of at least 10 characters, and an entry that is
  **unreconciled, not matched to a bank line, and from the current fiscal year.** A gift from a prior
  fiscal year is refused, and the Move button is not offered on it. Manage Ledger is a permission level,
  not a second approver.
- **Reopening a Foundation month hides member statements.** Reopening a closed Foundation reconciliation
  session hides the Foundation's monthly statements from members, from that month onward, until the
  session is closed again. The checklist says so before you start. Moving the gift itself does not change
  which months are ready to send.
- **If the Foundation's statement for that month was already sent to the board,** it will read as changed
  and you will be offered a corrected resend. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **A move cannot be undone.** Money in the Activity Fund leaves only through a minuted sweep. If the
  receipt was already sent, the moved gift can also no longer be deleted, and correcting it later is a
  refund entry. If you move the wrong entry and no receipt was sent, delete it and enter the gift on
  the correct register.
- **The receipt is kept as issued.** The ledger does not decide whether a receipt from the Foundation is
  still the right document for a gift the Foundation's books no longer carry. Confirm with whoever
  advises the club on tax matters, and mention it at the next board meeting.
- **Reconcile the Club side.** The moved gift is not reconciled on the Club's books until you match it to the
  deposit line in the Club's reconciliation session.
- **Totals shift.** The Foundation's income totals fall by the gift. When you record the sweep, the
  Foundation's totals count the transfer-in as income, as they do for every sweep. The Foundation side
  of a sweep still has no donor and no acknowledgment letter.
- **The other direction is still delete and re-enter.** If a Club gift's cash actually landed in the
  Foundation's bank account, delete the Club entry (it asks for a reason) and enter the gift on the
  Foundation's register, on the bank account the money landed in. The Delete dialog on Club income
  entries now says so. Moving from the Club to the Foundation is not available.

#### Fixes
- **Reconciliation now refuses a mismatched entry.** Matching a ledger entry to a bank line re-checks, at the moment of saving, that the entry still belongs to that bank account and is still posted; if another admin moved it in between, the match is refused instead of silently landing on the wrong account. Closing a reconciliation also refuses if any matched entry sits on a different bank account, and names the entries. Found during testing of this release; before it, such a mismatch could be closed without a warning.

- Gift acknowledgments now remember which entity issued them, so a receipt keeps naming its issuer
  wherever the gift's entry is later booked. Existing acknowledgments were filled in automatically.
- **Record sweep now** no longer prefills the memo with the wrong date after about 8 pm Eastern, and no longer says "moved"
  for an entry that was never moved.
