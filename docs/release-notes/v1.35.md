## 1.35.0 — 2026-07-22

### Enhancement: Treasury Guide Bank-Transition Steps & Menu Order

**Value:** The Treasurer's Guide had a thin version of the annual "change the
bank account when officers turn over" procedure, and the Treasury menu listed
its screens in an order that didn't match how often a treasurer actually
reaches for them.

**Changes:**
- The guide's **Treasurer Transition — Bank Account** section now spells out
  the full procedure: when to go (as soon as practical after July 1), who
  should attend (prior-year Treasurer, current Account Rep, incoming
  Treasurer, President, and optionally Secretary), what to bring (the minutes
  electing the new officers, plus two forms of ID per signer), and the tasks
  to complete at the branch — assign the new Account Rep (the Treasurer),
  make the Treasurer the primary signer, change the account's mailing address
  to the new Account Rep, destroy the old debit cards, and issue new cards
  (one per account) to the incoming Treasurer and President.
- The **Treasury menu** is reordered most-used to least-used: Ledger, Dues,
  Reconciliation, Reports, Compliance, Donors, Ledger Settings, and the
  User's Guide last.
