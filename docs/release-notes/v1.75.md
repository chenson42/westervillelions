## 1.75.0 — 2026-09-04

### Feature: Site-wide review overhaul

**Value:** A full critical review of the live site (nonprofit best practices, modern UX,
usability, mobile, performance) surfaced a set of defects and gaps. This release fixes all of
them in five coordinated passes, making the public site faster, more trustworthy for donors,
and far friendlier on phones.

#### What's New

**Fixed defects**
- The past-events page works (it previously crashed with a server error) and is now linked
  from the events calendar
- Mistyped or outdated links now show a friendly lion-themed "page not found" instead of
  bouncing visitors to the member login
- The site's Open Sans brand font now actually loads for visitors (it had been silently
  blocked since launch)
- The site has a proper icon in browser tabs, bookmarks, and phone home screens
- Shared event links now unfurl with their image on Facebook and other platforms

**Faster everywhere**
- Public pages are now served from a global cache instead of being rebuilt for every visitor —
  noticeably faster loads and lower hosting usage
- Event photos moved out of the page itself into properly cached, optimized images: the
  homepage shrank by two-thirds, the events page by more than half
- Modern AVIF image format enabled site-wide

**Better on phones**
- Every small touch target fixed: map/call links on the eyeglass drop-off list, footer links
  and social icons, newsletter form, testimonial carousel, and the donation window's close
  button all now meet the 44px touch standard
- The newsletter form no longer triggers the iOS zoom jump
- The events calendar is grouped by month with sticky headers
- The leadership page shows a compact two-column grid with tidy initials in place of giant
  placeholder silhouettes
- Events with no sign-ups yet invite you to "Be the first to sign up"

**Donor and member trust**
- New Privacy Policy page, linked from the footer and every public form
- The donate page now names the Westerville Lions Club Foundation as the 501(c)(3) recipient,
  shows its EIN for donor verification, and includes check-by-mail instructions right on the
  page
- A live impact band on the donate page and homepage: giving over the last two fiscal years,
  grant count, and estimated lifetime giving since 1928 — figures drawn from the club's own
  ledger
- The membership page now states annual dues (pulled live from the dues settings), explains
  what happens after you apply, and offers "visit a meeting first" as a first-class option

#### New Routes

- `GET /privacy` — Privacy Policy
- `GET /events/past` — public past-events listing
- `GET /api/public/events/[id]/image` — cached event image delivery
- `GET /api/events/[id]/viewer-context` — signed-in personalization for event pages
- `GET /api/public/campaigns/member-only` — member-only campaign listing for signed-in visitors
