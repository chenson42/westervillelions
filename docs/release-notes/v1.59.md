## 1.59.0 — 2026-08-08

### Feature: Ledger Category Management

**Value:** Categories are the backbone of the books — every transaction and every budget line hangs off
one — but there was no way to manage them. Renaming a mislabelled category, retiring one the club no
longer uses, or consolidating two that meant the same thing all required someone to write a one-off
script against the live database. In the past week alone that happened three times: relabelling
"Program supplies" as "Bags to Benches" after the budget committee couldn't find the benches budget,
and folding two duplicate pairs together where one name was budgeted and the other was where the money
actually went. This puts those operations in Settings, with guardrails a script never had.

#### What's New

- **A category manager** at Settings → Categories, organised by entity, fund, and income/expense, with
  search and filtering across the club's 100-plus categories.
- **Rename, and see what it affects first.** Before a rename commits, it tells you how many
  transactions and budget lines will relabel and which fiscal years are involved — including closed
  ones. A category name is a label, not a figure, so renames apply across history; you just see the
  scope before agreeing to it.
- **Retire a category** so it stops appearing when entering new transactions, without disturbing any
  history already recorded against it. Retired categories can be brought back — retiring one by mistake
  is not permanent.
- **Merge two categories** where one was budgeted and the other was used. Merging moves budget lines to
  the surviving category and refuses, with the reason stated, when it would be unsafe: if the category
  being absorbed has transactions of its own, if both already have a budget in the same year, or if it
  would touch a fiscal year the club has already closed out. A budgeted amount from a past year stays
  as the board approved it.
- **Edit the giving and 990 settings** on a category, with the dollar impact shown before you change
  whether a category counts toward the club's published giving totals.
- **An audit record** of every rename, merge, retirement, and setting change — who did it, when, and
  what changed from what.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `ledger.manage` | Viewing and managing categories |

No new permission key.

#### New Routes

- `GET /admin/ledger/settings/categories` — the category manager
- `GET /api/admin/ledger/categories` — list and filter
- `PATCH /api/admin/ledger/categories/[id]` — rename, retire, reinstate, and settings
- `GET /api/admin/ledger/categories/[id]/impact` — what a change would affect
- `POST /api/admin/ledger/categories/merge` — preview and apply a merge

#### Known Limits

- The audit record is written but has no viewer yet — reading it currently requires database access.
  A history view is the next planned addition.
- Merging is limited to the current fiscal year. Categories whose only remaining budget lines sit in a
  closed year cannot be merged, by design.
- A merge does not adjust the surviving category's budgeted amount; set that on the budget itself.
