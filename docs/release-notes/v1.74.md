## 1.74.0 — 2026-09-04

### Feature: Type-to-find search in the admin menu

**Value:** The admin area has grown past twenty sections; finding the right one meant scanning
the whole sidebar. Now it works like the search in your phone's Settings app.

#### What's New

- A search box at the top of the admin sidebar filters the menu live as you type
- Matching is forgiving — it finds partial and scattered letters ("emq" finds Email Queue),
  and it knows synonyms: typing "money" surfaces the Ledger areas, "facebook" surfaces Social
  Requests, "990" surfaces Compliance
- Matched letters are highlighted; sections with no matches disappear until you clear the
  search (Escape or the ✕ clears it)
- Search only ever narrows what your permissions already let you see

### Enhancement: Event links now always unfurl with an image when shared

**Value:** Pasting an event link into a text message, Facebook, or a group chat should expand
into a rich preview — picture, title, description — the way news links do.

**Changes:**
- Every event link now unfurls with an image: the event's own picture when it has one, the
  club's brand photo when it doesn't (previously, events without a picture unfurled as bare
  text — which was all of them)
- X/Twitter previews now show the event's image instead of the generic site image
- The admin event form's image field now explains the wide-banner crop, the recommended
  minimum size, and that the image doubles as the shared-link preview

## 1.74.1 — 2026-09-04

### Enhancement: Downtown Westerville returns to the homepage hero

**Value:** The hero's photo of uptown Westerville had been buried under a nearly opaque blue
overlay for months, and an earlier cleanup removed it entirely. After comparing options
side by side, the photo is back — actually visible this time.

**Changes:**
- The hero now shows the downtown Westerville photo under a light blue wash, chosen from a
  side-by-side comparison of overlay strengths
- The image is served optimized (compressed and sized to the visitor's screen) instead of
  the full-size original the old hero downloaded

## 1.74.2 — 2026-09-04

### Enhancement: Homepage and Mission page each get their own job

**Value:** After the homepage redesign, the homepage's "Our Service Areas" section and the
Mission page's "How We Serve" section showed the same eight causes with near-identical
wording — a visitor clicking through from the homepage landed on a copy of what they had
just read. The two pages now work together instead of repeating each other.

**Changes:**
- The homepage's "Our Service Areas" grid still shows all eight causes, but as compact
  icon-and-title tiles that act as a table of contents — each links to that cause's full
  description on the Mission page
- The Mission page remains the deep page, keeping the full description for every cause
- Both pages now use the same hand-drawn cause icons in brand blue; the Mission page's
  emoji (which rendered differently on every device) are replaced with the matching icons
- The Mission page's causes now lead with Community Service and Youth Programs, matching
  the homepage's community-forward ordering
- All existing links to individual causes (for example, from the homepage tiles) continue
  to land on the right section of the Mission page
