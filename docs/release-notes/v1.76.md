## 1.76.0 — 2026-09-09

### Feature: Browsing and searching the club's meeting minutes

**Value:** The club's meeting minutes going back to June 2024 are now in the member portal —
49 sets of minutes covering both board and general meetings, including every motion and action
item recorded in them. That much history made the old Club Records page unusable: a single
undivided list, with search results that looked exactly like the list itself. This release makes
the archive something you can actually navigate.

#### What's New

**Browse by Lions year**

- Club Records now has a row of year pills — `2023-24`, `2024-25`, `2025-26`, `2026-27` — and
  opens on the current Lions year. Years run July to June, matching how the club actually
  governs itself and how dues, budgets and the treasurer's reports already label a year.
- Each pill shows how many sets of minutes it holds, so you can see at a glance where the
  history is. The counts follow whichever filter you have on: switch between Board and General
  and the numbers change to match what you'll actually get.
- Early in a Lions year, before many meetings have happened, the current year is naturally
  sparse. Rather than showing you a bare empty page, Club Records tells you the year is still
  young and offers a one-click link to the most recent year that does have minutes.
- An "All years" pill shows the whole archive at once.

**Search that gives you context**

- Search results now show an excerpt of the text that matched, with your search term
  highlighted — so you can tell which meeting you actually want without opening each one.
- Every result says *where* it matched: in the minutes text, in a motion, or in an action item.
  Searching a member's name will show you the motion they moved, not just a date.
- Search looks across every year regardless of which year pill you have selected, so you never
  have to guess which year a decision was recorded in. It still respects the Board / General
  filter.

**Search now looks like search**

- Searching gives you a distinct view: a "Results for…" heading, a count of what was found, and
  a Clear search link that returns you to exactly the year and meeting type you were browsing
  before. Previously search results were rendered identically to the ordinary list, with no
  indication you were looking at search results at all.

#### Notes on the imported minutes

The historical minutes were transcribed from the club's archived PDFs and are faithful to those
documents, including their inconsistencies:

- Some sets of minutes record motions without naming who moved them; those show "Not recorded"
  rather than a guessed name.
- A few records have no attendance count, because the original attendance grid could not be
  read reliably from the scan.
- Where a document's own header disagreed with its filename, the header was used, corroborated
  against the neighbouring month's minutes.

## 1.76.1 — 2026-09-09

### Fix: iPhone receipt photos upload again in Chrome and Firefox

**Value:** Since 2026-09-04, attaching an iPhone photo of a receipt in The Ledger failed
for anyone using Chrome or Firefox — and the error message blamed the network, so the real
cause was invisible. Safari was unaffected. This restores it.

**What happened:** iPhones save photos in HEIC format, which Chrome and Firefox cannot read
on their own, so the site converts them in the browser. A security tightening in v1.76.0's
predecessor removed a browser permission that the converter quietly depended on, and the
converter stopped loading entirely.

**Changes:**
- Receipt photos in HEIC format convert and attach again in every supported browser
- The security tightening is kept: the restored permission is narrower than the one
  originally removed, allowing only the image converter to run and nothing else
- The dependency is now documented in the configuration itself, so a future security pass
  won't remove it again unknowingly

## 1.76.2 — 2026-09-10

### Fix: three small admin defects found during a code-quality sweep

**Value:** Restoring a dormant code-quality check surfaced several real problems that had
been invisible. None were reported by anyone — they were the kind of rough edge people
work around without mentioning.

**Changes:**
- The acknowledgment queue in The Ledger now shows dates as "Aug 15, 2026" like every
  other table, instead of the raw `2026-08-15`. The formatting had been written but never
  connected. It also would have displayed the wrong day for some viewers depending on
  their timezone; that is fixed too.
- Toggling a permission on the roles screen now shows a "Saving…" indicator and disables
  the checkboxes until it completes. Previously there was no feedback at all, and rapid
  clicking could send overlapping requests.
- If a file upload finished sending but failed on the final step, retrying now re-sends
  only that last step instead of re-uploading the whole file. For a 25MB club document
  on a slow connection, that is the difference between a moment and starting over.
