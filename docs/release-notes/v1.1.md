## 1.1.1 — 2026-03-26

### Defect Fix: Newsletter Export List Name

**Problem:** Zeffy newsletter export tagged subscribers under "Lions Members" instead of a newsletter-specific list, making it impossible to distinguish newsletter subscribers from club members in Zeffy.
**Root Cause:** Hardcoded list name was copied from the members export without being updated.
**Fix:** Changed the `Lists` field in the newsletter export to `"Newsletter Subscribed"`.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-03-26

### Enhancement: Public Site Content, SEO, UX, and Leadership

**Value:** Improves search visibility, corrects outdated content, and makes the site more compelling to prospective members.

**Changes:**

- **Leadership section on About page** — Dynamically pulls board members from the "Board of Directors" group with position-ordered display (President first)
- **Public leadership API** — New `GET /api/public/leadership` endpoint returns board group members without auth
- **Donate URL consolidation** — Full donate/campaigns page moved to `/donate`; `/campaigns` now redirects there. All nav, footer, and sitemap references updated
- **Google Maps embed** — Meeting location on the Contact page now includes an embedded map for The Landings (350 County Line Rd W, Westerville OH)
- **Newsletter pitch** — Updated copy from "Stay in the Loop" to "Be Part of Something Bigger" with clearer value proposition
- **Breadcrumb schema** — JSON-LD BreadcrumbList added to About, Mission, Events, Join, Contact, and Donate pages for improved SERP display
- **Homepage og:image** — Social share image updated to `hero-bg.jpg` (1200×630) replacing the small logo
- **Meeting info corrected** — About page updated to 1st and 3rd Thursdays at The Landings (was Wednesdays at Westerville Community Center)
- **Events page title** — Nav label renamed from "What We Do" to "Events"
- **Mission & Service consolidation** — Mission and Our Causes merged into a single page
