## 1.88.0 — 2026-10-02

Two things the treasurer asked for ship together. The first lets the next treasurer do the job without being an
administrator. The second makes a reimbursement check show up in bank reconciliation, and lets the treasurer fix
one that was entered wrong.

### Feature: The treasurer role covers the Treasury area

**Value:** A new treasurer no longer needs the Administrator role for the Treasury work. The corrections,
categories, settings, dues, budgeting and the Email Queue all come with the Treasurer role. Approving
disbursements and budgets still comes with the Board role.

#### What's New

- **The Treasurer role now covers the Treasury area, except Approvals.** That includes reopening or
  discarding a reconciliation, editing a fund, managing categories, changing settings, adding a compliance
  filing, waiving a receipt, moving or deleting a settled entry, and deleting a donor. Approvals stay with the
  Board role, on purpose: approving is a board act, not a treasurer one. Someone who holds both roles has both.
- **The treasurer can open the Email Queue.** A new permission, Manage Email Queue, lets the treasurer see
  which emails went out and retry one that failed. It is bound to the Treasurer and Administrator roles by
  default.
- **The Email Queue no longer shows passwords or reset links.** Stored emails that carried a password-reset link
  or a temporary password now show those parts hidden, for every viewer, including administrators. A failed
  email that is retried still goes out exactly as it was written, so nothing a member receives has changed.
- **Existing administrators keep Email Queue access.** Nothing changes for them and nothing needs to be done.
  For one release, anyone who already had the "manage users" permission also keeps Email Queue access.
- **Budget committee members can add a category** from the budget page. Before this, the button was shown but
  the save failed. Two settings stay with the Manage Ledger permission: "Counts toward reported community
  giving" turned off, and a Form 990 line. If a budget committee member sets either, the message says so and
  nothing is saved.
- **Event announcers can open the Events list.** The navigation already promised this; the page now agrees.
- **Three changes now leave a record.** Editing a fund, deleting a donor and changing ledger settings each write
  an audit entry. There is no screen to read them yet.

#### What the treasurer should know

- **Sign out and back in after a role change.** A person's roles are read when they sign in. If a page still
  sends you to "access pending" right after you sign back in, wait about a minute and reload.
- **Anyone who holds the Treasurer role today gains these abilities when this release is deployed,** including
  the second non-admin holder. Check that this is what you want.
- **Board membership is what gives a treasurer the admin dashboard.** A treasurer who is also on the Board gets the
  dashboard and Approvals. An account with only the Treasurer role opens the Treasury pages directly, but the
  **Admin** link at the top of the site shows "access pending" until that person is added to the Board group.
- **Not covered yet:** setting up bank accounts, opening balances and funds (B-109), and an officer-handover
  screen (B-110). Those still need an administrator.
- **Manage Ledger is a permission level, not a second approver.** It does not change who approves what. The
  approval threshold, the self-approval block, reconciliation and the monthly statement to the board are
  still the real controls.

### Feature: Paid reimbursements can be reconciled and corrected

**Value:** A reimbursement the treasurer pays by check now shows up in bank reconciliation like any other
check, and if something was entered wrong it can be fixed in the register with a recorded reason, with no
developer needed.

#### What's New

- **Paying a reimbursement now records the bank account and the check number.** Mark Paid asks which bank
  account the money came out of (the entity's default is preselected) and takes the check number when the
  payment method is Check. The bank's check line can then be matched to the entry in reconciliation. The
  member's description is no longer overwritten: the new "Register description" starts as what the member
  wrote, and the check number has its own field.
- **The two October 1 reimbursements can be repaired from the register.** Reimbursements paid before this
  release have no bank account, so the bank's check line could never be matched to them. The register now shows
  "No bank account" on those entries with an **Add bank account** button. It adds the account (and the check
  number, if you have it) and nothing else. Once repaired, the entry appears in the open September
  reconciliation, where you can match it. The repair is recorded under Recent corrections.
- **Paid reimbursements are labelled in the register.** The lock label reads "Paid reimbursement" instead of
  "Approved", and the advice is now accurate: use **Correct**, not a refund entry.
- **Correct a paid reimbursement.** Change its category, budget line, date, payment method, check number,
  description or bank account, with a reason of 10 to 500 characters. The amount, the fund and who was paid
  cannot be changed here. The date and bank account can change only while the entry is not matched to a bank line
  or reconciled. Entries that were reconciled, or dated in an earlier fiscal year, need the Manage Ledger
  permission, and a statement already sent to the board is flagged the same way it is for a move.
- **Recent corrections** on the Compliance page now also lists these repairs and corrections, with who, when,
  what changed and why.
- **Creating an expense from a bank line warns about a duplicate.** If the bank line is a debit and a paid
  reimbursement of the same amount is waiting, the dialog says so and offers **Use that entry instead**.
  Creating a new entry anyway needs you to tick "This is a different payment." The match picker also explains
  when a paid reimbursement is missing from its list because it has no bank account yet, and offers to add one.
- **A change that would hide a monthly statement from members is refused.** Moving an unreconciled entry to an earlier
  month, or changing its method away from Check, can take that month's statement off the members' page until the entry
  is reconciled. The correction says so and does not go through.
- **Amount and fund cannot be corrected this way.** A wrong amount still needs a refund entry (or a new request);
  a wrong fund is a move, not a correction. The Treasury Guide covers both.
- **The Treasury Guide** explains correcting a paid reimbursement, adding the bank account, and what to do if
  the same payment was already booked in a closed period.

#### What the treasurer should know

- **Nothing about a closed reconciliation can change through Correct.** The bank account and date are locked once an
  entry is matched. To fix a wrong account on a matched entry, unmatch it, correct it, and match it again.
- **Existing reimbursement entries whose description holds a check number were not changed.**
- **You cannot repair or correct the entry for your own reimbursement request.** Another reviewer must do it.
- **Mark Paid sends an email to the member.** That is unchanged.

### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `email_queue.manage` | Opening the Email Queue and retrying a failed email. New. Bound to the Administrator and Treasurer roles. For one release, anyone with `admin.users` also has access. |
| `ledger.manage` | Now bound to the Treasurer role as well as Administrator. Reopening or discarding a reconciliation, categories, settings, fund edits, donor deletes, and correcting a reconciled or prior-year paid reimbursement |
| `ledger.record` | Paying a reimbursement with a bank account, adding a bank account to a paid reimbursement, and correcting an unreconciled current-year one |
| `budget.edit` | Adding a category from the budget page (the two manage-only settings still need `ledger.manage`) |
| `ledger.view` | Reading Recent corrections on the Compliance page |

Sign out and back in after a role change; roles are read at sign-in.
