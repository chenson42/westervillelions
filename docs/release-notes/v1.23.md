## 1.23.0 — 2026-06-25

### Feature: The Ledger — Increment 4 ("Reports & 990-Prep Export")

**Value:** Gives the treasurer the year-end financial picture in one place and the two exports needed to close the books and prepare the federal return — without re-keying anything into a spreadsheet.

#### What's New

- **Financial report** (`/admin/ledger/reports`) — a per-entity, per-fiscal-year consolidation: each fund's opening balance, itemized income, itemized expenses, and ending balance, plus entity totals (gross receipts, net) and which federal return is owed. Pending (unapproved) disbursements are shown separately as encumbered, not mixed into the totals.
- **Transaction-ledger CSV export** — every transaction for an entity and year (date, fund, category, payee, amount, status, reconciled, method, memo) for the annual audit.
- **990-prep worksheet CSV** — income and expenses aggregated by simplified IRS Form 990-EZ line groups (internal fund transfers excluded so gross receipts aren't double-counted), with the determined return and a clear cash-basis disclaimer that it's a preparation worksheet, not the filed return.
- Both exports are **safe to open in Excel/Sheets** — any field that could be read as a spreadsheet formula (a payee or memo starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, or `@`) is neutralized, preventing CSV-injection.

#### Permissions

No new permission key. Viewing the report uses `ledger.view` (board included); the CSV exports are gated on `ledger.view` or the existing `reports.export`.

#### New Routes

- `GET /admin/ledger/reports`
- `GET /api/admin/ledger/export?entity=&fy=&type=transactions|990prep`
