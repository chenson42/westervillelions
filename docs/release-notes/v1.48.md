## 1.48.0 — 2026-07-29

### Feature: Budgeting Page Restructure

**Value:** After the FY2026 budget meeting, adding and removing lines live in front of the board was
a fight — the "add category" control was buried at the bottom, adding a beneficiary line dumped it
onto the wrong cause, and removes often took several clicks to register. This reworks the budgeting
page so it's actually usable in a meeting, and makes the printable version detailed enough to mail
out for review.

#### What's New

- **Income and Expense sections** under each fund, each with its own header — no more one flat
  interleaved list.
- **"+ Add category" moved to each section header**, where it's easy to find.
- **"+ Add cause"** on giving-eligible expense categories, and **"+ Add line item"** on each cause —
  a new line now inherits the cause it's added under, so the old per-line cause dropdown is gone and
  a line can no longer land in the wrong group.
- **Explicit remove at every level** — line item, whole cause, and whole category — that works on
  the **first click** (fixes the multi-click remove bug, a focus/commit race).
- **Removing a line item is instant with a quiet Undo**; removing a **cause** or a **category**
  asks for confirmation and names what it takes with it. Every removal stays **reversible** (a
  Restore control) until the budget is approved & locked.
- **The printable/mailable budget worksheet** now shows the full cause and beneficiary-line detail
  (with per-cause subtotals), not just category totals — so it can be mailed to members and the
  board for review.

#### Permissions
