## 1.62.0 — 2026-08-09

### Feature: Meeting Minutes

**Value:** The club's minutes have lived wherever the secretary kept them. This puts them in the member
portal — written, approved, searchable, and readable by any member — with the record of what the club
decided kept alongside the meetings it decided them at.

#### What's New

- **A place to write and keep minutes**, for both general and board meetings. Minutes open on the next
  meeting, and each meeting links to the previous meeting's approved minutes — which is the order of
  business anyway, since approving the last set is how a meeting starts.
- **Written the way a secretary actually works.** Paste straight from Word and the formatting comes
  across — headings, bold, lists, links, tables. Fonts, colours and images do not carry over, which the
  editor says up front rather than leaving you to discover.
- **The parts that matter recorded properly:** how many were present, motions with who moved and
  seconded them and how they went, action items with an owner and a due date, and the discussion in
  your own words.
- **Who took the minutes is recorded separately from who typed them in** — so a secretary's minutes
  stay the secretary's minutes even when someone else enters them later.
- **Draft until approved.** Minutes stay a draft until the club approves them, and the record shows
  which they are. Approving can be undone if something needs correcting.
- **Send them out when they're ready.** After saving, you're offered the chance to email them, with a
  short note of your own if you want one. Board minutes can go to the board at any point — including as
  a draft for review, clearly marked as one. Club-wide minutes go out only once approved, so members
  never receive a version that later changes.
- **Searchable**, and past minutes stay browsable.
- Any member can read any minutes. Deleting is limited to administrators, and nothing is ever really
  erased — minutes are kept permanently.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `minutes.manage` | Writing, editing, and approving minutes |
| `minutes.delete` | Removing a set of minutes |

A new **Notetaker** role carries these, intended for the secretary. Note this is assigned by an
administrator — it does not follow the office automatically, so it needs moving when the secretary
changes.

#### New Routes

- `GET /members/records` — minutes for members
- `GET /admin/minutes` — writing and approving minutes

### Enhancement: A tidier member portal

**Value:** The portal home had grown to eight tiles.

**Changes:**
- Six tiles now: Directory, Events, Groups, Club Records, Profile, and Club Finances.
- Dues and Reimbursements sit under Profile; Financial Statements and Community Impact under Club
  Finances.
- **Every existing link still works.** Nothing moved — only the way in changed — so saved bookmarks and
  links sent round in past emails all still open the right page.

---

### Enhancement: Test emails can no longer reach the club

**Value:** While this release was being tested, a test message reached the club's real mailing list.
That should not have been possible from a test environment, and now it isn't.

**Changes:**
- Mail addressed to the club's or the board's mailing list is never delivered from anywhere except the
  live site. It is recorded as blocked instead, so the feature can still be tested end to end without
  anything reaching members.
- The email log now shows blocked messages and lets you **view any message exactly as it would arrive**,
  which is also the easiest way to check how something looks before it goes out.
