## 1.91.0 — 2026-10-11

### Feature: Santa's Workshop prototype (unlisted)

**Value:** A member's approved proposal, "A Visit to Santa's Workshop", has preschoolers send a stuffed animal on a pretend train ride to the North Pole. Before the real sign-up is built, the project chair can tap through a working preview and confirm how it should look, feel and work.

#### What's New

- Seven screens at an unlisted preview address: the parent's sign-up page and "you're booked" screen, following the stuffy's journey, the memory book, the volunteer conductor's check-in list, and Saturday's photo station. The memory-book screen lets you compare the three delivery options (paperless, paperless with a printed keepsake card, or a printed book) side by side.
- The preview is not linked from the site and is hidden from search engines. Everything on it is sample content: nothing is saved, no one is emailed, and no payment is taken. Each screen says it is a prototype.
- An art brief page lists every picture the page needs, with the size and shape of each, so the artwork can be supplied.
- Real registration comes in a later release.
