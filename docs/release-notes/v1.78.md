## 1.78.1 — 2026-09-25

### Defect Fix: Event signup rosters are reachable again

**Problem:** Members who went to look up who had signed up for a recurring event could not find
the list. On the Farmers Market — a weekly series running May through September — the signup
section still appeared, but with no dates under it and no names. The event had also quietly
moved itself from "Upcoming Events" to "Past Events," and the final week of the season could not
be signed up for at all. It had worked earlier in the season.

**Root Cause:** Two independent defects that only combined at the end of a series.

- The recurrence end date is stored at midnight, but each occurrence inherits the event's own
  start time. A 12:30 PM occurrence falling on the last day therefore counted as *after* the
  series end, so the final occurrence of every recurring event was silently never generated.
  This also made the whole event look finished, which is why it jumped to "Past Events."
- The public event page only ever built occurrences from *now* forward, so an occurrence — and
  the roster attached to it — disappeared the moment its start time passed. The rest of the
  application (the admin event page, the signup API, the calendar export) already walked the
  series from its beginning; this one page did not.

Neither was a recent regression. Both had been present since the feature shipped and only ever
bite at the end of a series, and the Farmers Market was the club's first series to reach its end
date.

**Fix:**
- The series end date is now inclusive of the whole final day, so the last occurrence of a
  recurring event is generated, appears under "Upcoming Events," accepts signups, and is
  included in the calendar (.ics) download.
- Past occurrences and their rosters are now shown on the event page, behind a "Show N past
  dates" toggle so a long-running series does not bury the next upcoming date. Past dates are
  dimmed and marked **Closed**; signing up for one remains impossible.
- The toggle opens automatically when a series has no upcoming dates left, so a finished season
  does not look empty.
- The roster now shows a loading indicator while it is being fetched, and a **Retry** option if
  that fetch fails — previously a failure looked identical to "nobody signed up."

### Security: Signee names are no longer sent to signed-out visitors

**Background:** Event pages are public. While fixing the roster, we found that the list of
signee names was being embedded in the page's data for **every** visitor, including those not
signed in. Nothing displayed the names to a signed-out visitor, but the data was present in the
page source and could be read without signing in.

This was **not introduced by the fix above** — it already applied to upcoming occurrences. The
roster fix would have widened it to a series' entire history, so it was closed first.

**Changes:**
- Signee names are no longer loaded at all unless the viewer is a signed-in club member. The
  names are fetched separately after sign-in is confirmed, rather than being filtered out at
  display time.
- Access is now checked against **linked club membership**, not merely "someone is signed in."
  A signed-in account with no linked member record receives no names.
- Signup **counts** remain public, as before — only names are restricted.

**Testing:**
- [x] Signed-in member sees the full roster on both past and upcoming dates
- [x] Signed-out visitor receives no names anywhere in the page data
- [x] Signed-in but unlinked account receives no names
- [x] Final occurrence of a season-ending series generates, appears as upcoming, and accepts signups
- [x] Signing up for a past date is refused
- [x] Verified at 360px

## 1.78.0 — 2026-09-22

### Feature: Unlinked Gifts — find donations that have no donor attached

**Value:** Donors & Acknowledgments had one worklist, and it only ever showed gifts of $250 or
more, because that is the IRS threshold for a required written acknowledgment. A $40 donation
never appeared there, so there was nothing to click and it looked as though small gifts simply
could not be linked to a donor. They always could — from the fund register — but nothing on
the Donors page said so.

#### What's New

- **A new "Unlinked Gifts" tab** listing Foundation income that has no donor attached, at
  **any amount** and in **any income category** — including grants, sponsorships, race entries
  and pooled deposits, so an organisational donor record can be attached to them.
- **Internal transfers between the Club and the Foundation are excluded.** A transfer has no
  donor by definition; listing them would only add noise.
- **Scoped to a fiscal year by default**, with an All Years option, so the list stays short
  rather than becoming the long scroll it exists to replace.
- Linking works exactly as it does elsewhere, including creating a new donor without leaving
  the dialog.

**The Pending Acknowledgments queue is unchanged.** It still lists only gifts of $250 or more,
because that queue answers a different question — which donations legally require a written
acknowledgment. Small gifts appear on the new tab, never as an outstanding IRS obligation.

---

### Feature: Acknowledge a donation of any amount

**Value:** The Acknowledge button appeared only on gifts of $250 or more, so there was no way
to send a thank-you letter for a $20 donation. Sending one is perfectly legitimate — below
$250 a donor substantiates the gift with their own bank record, so the letter is a courtesy
rather than a requirement — and the letter text was already suitable, being a plain factual
receipt that never claims to be legally required.

- **The Acknowledge button now appears at any amount**, consistently in all three places it
  lives: the fund register, the new Unlinked Gifts tab, and a donor's own detail page.
- **Sub-$250 acknowledgments are labelled as a courtesy**, so it is clear at a glance whether
  you are sending a thank-you or discharging an IRS obligation.
- The acknowledgment-type wording no longer reads "($250+)", which was misleading once these
  became routine.

---

### Fix: the "Generate Letters…" link no longer disappears

**Value:** The link to letter generation was shown only when an unsent acknowledgment of $250
or more existed. If the only acknowledgment waiting to be sent was a smaller one, the link
vanished — so a letter could be recorded with no way to reach the screen that prints it. It is
now driven by whether any acknowledgment is unsent, regardless of amount.

---

### Known Limitation

An acknowledgment recorded against a category flagged as *not requiring acknowledgment* —
grants, race entries, event receipts, pooled fundraiser deposits — will **not** appear in
Generate Letters, at any amount. This behaviour predates this release and applies equally to
gifts over $250. Whether that flag should prevent a letter from ever being generated is a
policy question, tracked as backlog item B-63.
