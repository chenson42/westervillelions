## 1.78.0 — 2026-09-22

### Feature: Unlinked Gifts — find donations that have no donor attached

**Value:** Donors & Acknowledgments had one worklist, and it only ever showed gifts of $250 or
more, because that is the IRS threshold for a required written acknowledgment. A $40 donation
never appeared there, so there was nothing to click and it looked as though small gifts simply
could not be linked to a donor. They always could — from the fund register — but nothing on
the Donors page said so.

#### What's New

- **A new "Unlinked Gifts" tab** listing Foundation income that has no donor attached, at
  **any amount** and in **any income category** — including grants, sponsorships, race entries
  and pooled deposits, so an organisational donor record can be attached to them.
- **Internal transfers between the Club and the Foundation are excluded.** A transfer has no
  donor by definition; listing them would only add noise.
- **Scoped to a fiscal year by default**, with an All Years option, so the list stays short
  rather than becoming the long scroll it exists to replace.
- Linking works exactly as it does elsewhere, including creating a new donor without leaving
  the dialog.

**The Pending Acknowledgments queue is unchanged.** It still lists only gifts of $250 or more,
because that queue answers a different question — which donations legally require a written
acknowledgment. Small gifts appear on the new tab, never as an outstanding IRS obligation.

---

### Feature: Acknowledge a donation of any amount

**Value:** The Acknowledge button appeared only on gifts of $250 or more, so there was no way
to send a thank-you letter for a $20 donation. Sending one is perfectly legitimate — below
$250 a donor substantiates the gift with their own bank record, so the letter is a courtesy
rather than a requirement — and the letter text was already suitable, being a plain factual
receipt that never claims to be legally required.

- **The Acknowledge button now appears at any amount**, consistently in all three places it
  lives: the fund register, the new Unlinked Gifts tab, and a donor's own detail page.
- **Sub-$250 acknowledgments are labelled as a courtesy**, so it is clear at a glance whether
  you are sending a thank-you or discharging an IRS obligation.
- The acknowledgment-type wording no longer reads "($250+)", which was misleading once these
  became routine.

---

### Fix: the "Generate Letters…" link no longer disappears

**Value:** The link to letter generation was shown only when an unsent acknowledgment of $250
or more existed. If the only acknowledgment waiting to be sent was a smaller one, the link
vanished — so a letter could be recorded with no way to reach the screen that prints it. It is
now driven by whether any acknowledgment is unsent, regardless of amount.

---

### Known Limitation

An acknowledgment recorded against a category flagged as *not requiring acknowledgment* —
grants, race entries, event receipts, pooled fundraiser deposits — will **not** appear in
Generate Letters, at any amount. This behaviour predates this release and applies equally to
gifts over $250. Whether that flag should prevent a letter from ever being generated is a
policy question, tracked as backlog item B-63.
