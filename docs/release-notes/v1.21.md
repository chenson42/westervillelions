## 1.21.0 — 2026-06-25

### Feature: The Ledger — Increment 2 ("Controls" + Expense Reimbursement)

**Value:** Adds the financial controls a nonprofit board and auditor expect on top of the increment‑1 books: every large disbursement is board‑approved, the recorder can't approve their own spending, the Lions two‑fund firewall is enforced, and the books reconcile against the bank statement. It also gives members a self‑service way to get reimbursed for out‑of‑pocket expenses, with the receipt and board approval the IRS rules require.

#### What's New

- **Approvals workflow** — an expense over the board threshold (default $250) is recorded as **pending** and excluded from the fund balance (shown as an "encumbered" figure) until a board member approves it with a meeting‑minute reference. Rejections are recorded with a reason. A new **Approvals** screen lists everything awaiting the board.
- **Segregation of duties** — a new **board‑approver** role (`ledger.approve`, board members + admins). Treasurers record and pay but **cannot approve their own** disbursements; board members approve but don't record. Approved/rejected entries become immutable.
- **Two‑fund firewall** — the overview now flags any Activity→Administrative fund movement as a high‑severity breach (public‑fundraising money may never cover club admin costs).
- **Reconciliation** — mark transactions reconciled against the monthly bank statement (per‑row or "reconcile all displayed"); the overview warns about stale unreconciled entries.
- **Member expense reimbursements** — members submit a reimbursement request from the portal (amount, description, and a **required receipt**); the board approves or rejects (with reason), and the treasurer marks it paid, which posts the expense to the fund they assign. Members are emailed at each step and can edit or withdraw a request while it's still pending.
- **Receipt storage is pluggable** — production uses Vercel Blob; local development and tests use a zero‑config filesystem adapter, so the whole flow runs without any cloud setup. Receipts are private — served only through an authenticated proxy, never a public URL.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `ledger.approve` | Approve / reject pending disbursements and reimbursements (Admin, Board Member) |

Treasurers (`ledger.record`) record and pay but cannot approve. Members submit reimbursements with no special permission — gated to their own linked record.

#### New Routes

- `GET /admin/ledger/approvals`, `GET /admin/ledger/reimbursements`, `GET /members/reimbursements`
- `POST /api/admin/ledger/transactions/[id]/approve` · `…/reject` · `…/reconcile`
- `GET·PATCH /api/admin/ledger/reimbursements` (+ `/[id]`, `/[id]/receipt`)
- `GET·POST·PATCH·DELETE /api/members/reimbursements` (+ `/[id]`, `/[id]/receipt`, `/upload`)

#### Deployment Note

Receipts require **`BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN`** (and a Vercel Blob store) in production. Without it the app falls back to the ephemeral local filesystem and uploaded receipts are lost on redeploy — set it before opening reimbursements to members. Local development needs no configuration.
