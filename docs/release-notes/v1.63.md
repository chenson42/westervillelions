## 1.63.0 — 2026-08-09

### Feature: Governing Documents

**Value:** The club's Constitution & By-Laws have existed as a printed booklet, last revised in 1998,
kept in the secretary's record book. Members had no practical way to read the rules they operate under.
This puts the document in the member portal — readable, and with every future amendment recorded and
comparable against what came before.

#### What's New

- **The Constitution & By-Laws, readable in the portal**, for any signed-in member.
- **Every change is kept.** Nothing is edited in place and nothing is deleted — each save creates a new
  version, and the full history stays available. That history is the record of how the club's rules
  changed and who changed them.
- **Compare any two versions** and see exactly what changed, line by line, with unchanged passages
  collapsed so a small amendment doesn't hide in six hundred lines of unchanged text.
- **Corrections and amendments are treated differently**, because they are different. A correction — a
  typo, a broken cross-reference — takes effect immediately. An amendment to what the rules actually
  say is held pending until it is formally adopted, since amending the by-laws requires a vote.
- **Adopting an amendment records who adopted it, when, and optionally the meeting minutes carrying the
  vote** — and that link can be added later, since minutes are not approved until the following meeting.
- **It is always clear which text is in force.** The current version is labelled as the club's governing
  text of record; superseded versions and pending amendments are plainly marked as such.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `documents.manage` | Creating versions and adopting amendments |

Carried by the Notetaker role and by administrators. Any signed-in member can read the document and its
history. Pending amendments are visible only to those who can adopt them.

#### New Routes

- `GET /members/records/documents/[slug]` — read the document, with `/history` and `/compare`
- `GET /admin/documents` — create versions and adopt amendments

#### Note

The document is not yet published — that is a deliberate step, taken when the club is ready, and it is
the point at which the in-app text becomes the club's authoritative version rather than the 1998 scan.

### Security: Access control hardening

**Value:** Work on this feature surfaced a recurring weakness in how admin pages were protected, and
then a live gap it would have opened. Both are now closed, along with several latent ones found by
auditing every admin page rather than only the ones we knew about.

**Changes:**
- **Admin areas are now protected from a single source.** Previously the list of who may reach which
  admin area was maintained separately from the navigation, and the two drifted — five separate times a
  new area shipped without protection, each time locking out the very people it was built for. Both now
  come from the same place, so a new area is protected automatically.
- **A newsletter-subscriber page was found to have no permission check of its own**, relying entirely on
  the outer protection. Under the change above it would have become reachable by a narrower permission
  than intended, exposing subscriber names and email addresses. It now has its own check, on a dedicated
  permission held by the same people as before.
- **Every one of the club's admin pages was audited** for this same pattern; the gaps found are fixed.
- **A test now fails if a new admin page ships without a permission check**, so this cannot silently
  return.
- A membership page had also been protected by the wrong permission for some time, due to a
  pattern-matching flaw; it now uses its own.

No one loses access they previously had.
