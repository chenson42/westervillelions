## 1.52.0 — 2026-07-30

### Fix: A month's statement no longer waits on deposits that clear late

**Value:** The Monthly Statement of Financial Condition auto-publishes for a month once everything
dated on/before that month's last day is reconciled. But a deposit recorded near month-end that
doesn't hit the bank until the following month (e.g. a June 27 dues payment that lands on the July
statement) was holding the whole month back — June's Club statement wouldn't appear until those
deposits cleared weeks later. That's a normal month-end **timing difference**, exactly like an
outstanding check.

**Changes:**
- A **deposit-in-transit** (any posted, unreconciled income) is now treated the same as an
  **outstanding check** — it no longer gates the month. This makes the income side symmetric with the
  expense side, which already excluded outstanding checks.
- Reverses the earlier decision that kept received-check income gating (superseded by DECISION-059).
- **No figures change** — the statement's numbers were always correct; this only affects *when* a
  month becomes available.

### New: Unremitted Deposits panel

**Value:** Now that uncleared deposits no longer gate a month, they still need to stay visible so one
that never actually clears doesn't quietly disappear.

- A new **Unremitted Deposits** list on the ledger dashboard (`/admin/ledger`) — the income-side twin
  of the existing **Uncashed Checks** list — showing posted income deposits not yet reconciled, with
  an amber flag past 90 days. Same permissions as the dashboard (no new gate).

#### Follow-ups tracked
- Manual production check that June now appears for the Club once July's Administrative Checking
  statement is reconciled.
- `getDashboard()`/`unremittedDeposits` query-level unit coverage (pre-existing gap, sibling
  `uncashedChecks` untested too; covered by a new e2e spec).
