## 1.22.0 — 2026-06-25

### Feature: The Ledger — Increment 3 ("Compliance")

**Value:** Gives the treasurer and board a single place to stay on top of the club's and foundation's government filings, see which IRS return each entity owes, and not miss a deadline — the obligations that, if dropped three years running, cost a nonprofit its tax-exempt status. Built from the Lions Financial Transparency procedures.

#### What's New

- **Filings calendar** (`/admin/ledger/compliance`) — per entity, the annual and 5-year obligations seeded from the transparency doc: IRS 990-N, the Ohio AG Charitable Annual Report, the Ohio Unclaimed Funds report, the Statement of Continued Existence (every 5 years), and the internal treasurer's audit. Grouped into Overdue / Upcoming / Filed, as mobile-friendly cards. A treasurer marks each one **in progress** or **filed** (with a date + confirmation number).
- **990 determiner** — for each entity and fiscal year, the system shows which federal return is owed (990-N / 990-EZ / 990, or 990-PF if the Foundation is ever reclassified as a private foundation) based on gross receipts and assets, with a plain-language explanation. The asset figure is clearly labeled an estimate (entity cash balance) so it isn't mistaken for the filed number. A **high-severity warning** fires if a 990 has gone unfiled for three consecutive years (the auto-revocation threshold).
- **Standing compliance reminders** — the recurring rules a board must keep in view: raffles must give 50% to charity, no political endorsements, sales tax on taxable fundraising products, gaming-license rules, no informal "employment," social-media monitoring, and 7-year record retention.
- **Ledger settings screen** (`/admin/ledger/settings`) — admins can now configure the disbursement approval threshold, the reserve-warning threshold, the treasurer-bonded flag, and the philanthropy-dashboard visibility (previously seed-only).
- **Automatic year rollover** — each new fiscal year's filing checklist is generated from the prior year automatically, starting fresh (a filed 990 doesn't pre-fill next year's).

#### Permissions

No new permission key. Reuses the existing ledger tiers: `ledger.view` (read compliance — board included), `ledger.record` (mark filings filed — treasurers), `ledger.manage` (settings + add/edit/delete filings — admins).

#### New Routes

- `GET /admin/ledger/compliance`, `GET /admin/ledger/settings`
- `POST·PATCH·DELETE /api/admin/ledger/filings` (+ `/[id]`)
- `PATCH /api/admin/ledger/settings`
