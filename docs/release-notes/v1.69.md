## 1.69.0 — 2026-09-01

### Enhancement: Click-to-copy dollar figures on member financial reports

**Value:** Selecting a formatted dollar amount like "$17,927.83" by double-clicking only grabs part of
it in most browsers — the "$" and "," break word boundaries — leaving a click-drag or manual retyping
as the only way to copy a figure off the Statement of Financial Condition tables.

**Changes:**
- Every dollar amount on `/members/financial-reports/[entitySlug]/[month]` now copies its exact value
  to the clipboard on click, with a brief confirmation toast
- Print output is unaffected — the copy affordance is inert when printed/saved as PDF
