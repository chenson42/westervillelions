## 1.94.0 — 2026-10-12

### Feature: Santa's Workshop sessions and live registrations from Zeffy

**Value:** Santa's Workshop now has its own area in the admin menu. Admins and board members can set up a session, connect it to the club's Zeffy ticket form, and watch registrations appear on their own as families buy tickets, one entry for each stuffy with its drop-off window, snack choice and notes from the form. Volunteers no longer re-key anything from Zeffy.

#### What's New

- A session holds the event's dates, the three drop-off windows and the connected Zeffy form, and can be corrected at any time. A practice session can be set up for the dry run and a real one for the event.
- Registrations arrive within seconds of each Zeffy payment. Anything the form said that the club has not matched up yet is flagged for a look instead of being lost, refunds are marked rather than removed, and a Sync button catches anything that was missed, with a plain sentence saying why when something could not be processed.
- A go-live checklist on the session page shows what is still to do before registration opens.
- The club's Zeffy account now talks to the website directly, which later lets the White Cane page show a live total and lets donations flow into the books without a spreadsheet.
- The sign-up page has its artwork: the station scene, the blue train, the three step icons, the story picture and a link-preview card, in a hand-drawn picture-book style.

#### Permissions

- New `santas_workshop.manage`, granted to admins and board members. Board members must sign out and back in once after this release to see the new menu entry.

#### New Routes

- `GET /admin/santas-workshop` and its session pages — admin
- `POST /api/zeffy/webhook` — receives signed Zeffy payment events (no sign-in; the signature is the authentication)
