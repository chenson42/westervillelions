## 1.4.0 — 2026-04-06

### Feature: Google Groups Sync, Member Portal Groups, and Member Data Enrichment

**Value:** Group email addresses (e.g. `board@westervillelions.org`) now stay in sync with portal membership automatically. All active members are synced to `club@westervillelions.org` whenever membership changes. Members can browse groups and contact them by email from the portal. Member records now include full birthdates, addresses, gender, and spouse names from the LCI export.

---

#### Google Groups Sync

- **OAuth2 authentication** via a dedicated `api@westervillelions.org` service account using a refresh token — no service account key required
- Each portal group can be assigned an optional **email prefix** (e.g. `board` → `board@westervillelions.org`)
- Admin group edit form: email prefix input with `@westervillelions.org` suffix, **Sync Now** button, last-synced timestamp, and inline error display
- Sync creates the Google Group if it doesn't exist, then adds/removes members to match the portal roster
- **Sync reports changes**: toast notification lists added and removed email addresses (or confirms "already up to date")
- `POST /api/admin/groups/[id]/sync` — admin-only manual sync endpoint

#### Club-Wide Sync (`club@westervillelions.org`)

- All active members are synced to `club@westervillelions.org` automatically whenever a member is created, updated, or deleted
- **Sync club@** button on the admin Members page for manual full sync
- Sync report shows added/removed emails or confirms no changes needed
- `POST /api/admin/members/sync-club` — admin-only manual sync endpoint

#### Member Portal Groups Pages

- `/members/groups` — lists all groups with name, description, and contact email (if set)
- `/members/groups/[id]` — group detail page with prominent contact email card and member list with profile pictures
- **Groups** quick-link added to the member portal home (now 4 cards)

#### Member Data Enrichment

- `gender` and `spouse_name` columns added to the `members` table
- `pnpm db:update-member-details` command imports full addresses, complete birthdates (with year), gender, and spouse names from the LCI member detail export
- 47 members updated with complete data from the February 2026 LCI export
- Birthday display on the member portal now correctly parses full `YYYY-MM-DD` dates
