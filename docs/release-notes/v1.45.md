## 1.45.1 — 2026-07-28

### Defect Fix: Detailed Budget Lines Can Be Zeroed Out

**Problem:** On a giving category broken into cause/beneficiary lines, clearing a
line's amount to zero it out threw an error toast ("Enter an amount … or remove this
line") and refused to save — so a beneficiary you wanted to keep on the worksheet with
nothing budgeted couldn't be entered, whether starting a new line at $0 or zeroing an
existing one.

**Root Cause:** The cause-line editor treated an empty amount field as an error on both
the create and update paths, even though $0 is a valid budget amount everywhere below
it (the API, the query layer, and the database all accept it). Only a literal typed
"0" got through.

**Fix:** A cleared amount on a detailed line is now saved as a deliberate $0 (both when
adding a new line and when editing an existing one), and the field normalizes to
"0.00" after saving. Removing a line entirely is still done with its trash button, not
by emptying the box. Category total (= sum of its lines) and approve/lock are
unaffected.

**Testing:**
- [x] Typecheck and the full unit suite pass (695 tests)
- [ ] Manual: clear an existing cause line → saves as $0 and stays; add a new line and
  leave the amount blank → saves as $0; the trash button still removes a line

## 1.45.0 — 2026-07-28

A budget-meeting release: see at a glance whether each account balances, set
beneficiary-level budgets with last year's numbers on every line, remove a line
without losing it until you finalize, and the Administrative fund now always sorts
first for the Club.

### Feature: Budget-Balance Overview per Account

**Value:** Walking into a budget meeting, the first question is "does this account
balance?" This puts the answer at the top of every fund's report — with the dues-timing
wrinkle handled honestly.

#### What's New

- **A balance banner at the top of each fund report** (`/admin/ledger/<fund>/report`).
  It states, in plain language, whether the account is balanced — and it's
  fund-aware: the Administrative fund flags a real operating deficit, an activity
  (pass-through) fund checks that receipts and disbursements land within ~$100 of each
  other, and a charitable fund treats a planned drawdown of its balance as normal, not
  a problem.
- **Defaults to the most-recently-closed fiscal year.** Since the point is to judge a
  finished year, the report now opens on last fiscal year (the one that just ended
  6/30) instead of the in-progress year; the year selector still switches freely.
- **Handles dues paid early.** Members often pay next year's dues before the current
  year closes, which distorts a straight cash-basis income total. When a fund has dues
  activity, the banner shows both the cash-basis figure **and** an adjusted figure that
  credits each dues payment to the fiscal year it was actually *for* — with a one-line
  note explaining the difference. Funds with no dues activity just show the one number.

### Enhancement: Prior-Year Reference on Detailed (Cause) Budget Lines

**Value:** The prior-year Budget/Actual reference added in v1.44.0 stopped at the
category level. When you break a giving category into beneficiary lines (WARM,
Westerville Sharing & Caring, and so on), each line now carries its own history.

**Changes:**
- Each cause/beneficiary line shows last year's **Budget** and **Actual** next to its
  amount, matched by category + cause + beneficiary label — so you set WARM's number
  with WARM's own prior figures in front of you, not just the rolled-up "Hunger & Basic
  Needs" total.
- Prior **Actual** populates wherever last year had transactions tagged to that
  beneficiary; prior **Budget** populates once a beneficiary-level budget exists for the
  prior year (this year's detailed budget becomes next year's reference).

### Enhancement: Soft-Delete of Budget Lines

**Value:** Removing a budget line during a working session used to be immediate and
unrecoverable. Now a removal is reversible right up until you finalize.

**Changes:**
- Clearing a line marks it for removal and keeps it visible with a struck-through
  "deleted" treatment and a **Restore** toggle, instead of vanishing on the spot.
- Marked-for-removal lines are excluded from the fund's running budget totals and
  balance readout while you work, so the numbers reflect your intended budget.
- The lines are purged for good only when you **Approve & lock** the budget — the
  approve dialog tells you how many will be removed before you commit.

### Enhancement: Administrative Fund Ordered First (Club)

**Value:** The Club's Administrative fund is the one you reach for most; it now sorts
first everywhere instead of alphabetically.

**Changes:**
- Fund lists on both the budgeting page and The Ledger order by fund role
  (Administrative → Charitable → Activity → Scholarship), then by name — so the
  Administrative fund leads for the Club and the Charitable fund leads for the
  Foundation.

#### Permissions

_No new permission keys. All surfaces stay under the existing Ledger view/record/manage
gates; the balance overview is admin-only._

#### Database

- Adds a nullable `pending_delete_at` timestamp to budget lines (idempotent migration)
  to track soft-deleted-but-not-yet-finalized lines. No member-facing data change.
