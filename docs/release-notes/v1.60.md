## 1.60.2 — 2026-08-08

### Enhancement: Categories that never need a donor acknowledgment

**Value:** The acknowledgment queue listed every Foundation deposit of $250 or more, which swept in
things that are not a donor's gift at all — race registration deposits, pancake breakfast receipts, a
grant, and an internal transfer between the club's own accounts. Writing an acknowledgment letter for
"Rudolph Run Entry Receipts" would be wrong: that is a batch of many people's race
entries, not one donor's contribution. These recur every year, so dismissing them one at a time would
be a chore repeated forever.

**Changes:**
- A category can now be marked as **not needing donor acknowledgment**, from the category settings
  where its other options live. Gifts in that category stop appearing on the acknowledgment queue.
- The option appears only where it makes sense — income categories on the Foundation, which is the
  entity whose donations are tax-deductible.
- Five categories are marked automatically with this release: Rudolph Run registration and entry fees,
  Pancake Breakfast, Fundraising events, Grants received, and Transfer from Club. The queue drops from
  55 entries to the genuine outstanding letters.
- The change is recorded in the category history alongside the other settings, so it is clear who
  changed it and when.

This is separate from whether a category counts toward the club's giving totals — a category can do one
and not the other.

## 1.60.1 — 2026-08-08

### Defect Fix: Donor acknowledgments could not be completed

**Problem:** Recording an acknowledgment on a donation appeared to do nothing. The gift stayed on the
pending list looking exactly as it had before, the donor's name did not appear next to it, and the
donor's own record showed no gifts and nothing given. Trying again produced an error telling the
treasurer to use "Mark Sent" — an action with no button anywhere on that screen. The acknowledgment
workflow could not be finished from the site at all. Reported from real use on the club's first
recorded donor.

**Root Cause:** Three separate faults behind one symptom. Recording an acknowledgment linked the donor
to the acknowledgment but not to the donation itself, and everything that displays donors reads the
donation — so the donor looked unconnected everywhere it mattered. The pending list was never given
the information needed to tell "not yet acknowledged" apart from "acknowledged, letter not sent," so
it always offered the same button, and the one that would have completed the task was unreachable.

**Fix:** Recording an acknowledgment now links the donor to the donation in the same step, so the two
can no longer disagree. The pending list distinguishes the two states with different labels, and each
gift now offers the action that actually applies — "Record acknowledgment" for a new one, "Mark Sent"
once the letter has been written. Marking a letter sent clears the gift from the list, as expected.

Recording an acknowledgment remains deliberately separate from sending the letter: the IRS requires
the donor to receive it, so a gift stays on the list until the letter has gone out.

**Testing:**
- [x] A browser test now walks the whole path — add a donor, record an acknowledgment, see the status
      and button change, mark the letter sent, and watch the gift leave the list.
- [x] Verified that the donation itself carries the donor, which is what the original fault missed.
- [x] Full suite green, no regressions.

**Note:** The affected donation in the club's live books was corrected directly, so that donor's record
was already right before this release.

## 1.60.0 — 2026-08-08

### Feature: Budget context while entering a transaction

**Value:** Deciding whether to record a payment often means knowing whether the money is there — and
that meant leaving the entry form, opening the budget page, finding the category, and coming back. The
answer now appears where the decision is made.

#### What's New

- **Pick a category while entering a transaction and the budget appears** — what was budgeted, what has
  been used, and what remains.
- **It shows where this transaction lands you**, not just where you were: "$300 of $500 used — $500
  after this one," updating as you type the amount. No extra loading; the projection is calculated as
  you go.
- **Committed money counts.** Transactions entered but not yet cleared by the bank are included in what
  has been used, because money that is spent is spent whether or not it has cleared. Where pending
  money is involved the figure breaks it out, so it is clear what is counted. Note this is deliberately
  broader than the fiscal reports, which count only cleared transactions.
- **Choose a specific budget line and you see that line first**, with the whole category underneath —
  so you know both whether the line is covered and how the category is tracking.
- **Income reads as progress, not consumption**: "$10,000 of $10,000 expected — 100% received." Passing
  an income target is good news and is never flagged as a problem.
- **Back-dating works correctly.** Entering a June expense in August shows last fiscal year's budget,
  because the figures follow the transaction's date rather than today's.
- **A category with no budget says so plainly** rather than showing zeroes that could be mistaken for
  being at the limit.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `budget.view` *or* `ledger.manage` | Seeing budget figures while entering a transaction |

Recording transactions is gated separately; someone able to record but not to see budgets will simply
not see this panel.

#### New Routes

- `GET /api/admin/ledger/budget-context` — budget and spending for one fund and fiscal year

#### Known Limits

- The posted-and-pending breakdown appears only when there is pending money to break out.
- The reimbursement "mark paid" dialog does not show this context yet, though it uses the same budget
  line picker.
