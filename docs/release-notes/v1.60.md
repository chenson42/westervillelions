## 1.60.0 — 2026-08-08

### Feature: Budget context while entering a transaction

**Value:** Deciding whether to record a payment often means knowing whether the money is there — and
that meant leaving the entry form, opening the budget page, finding the category, and coming back. The
answer now appears where the decision is made.

#### What's New

- **Pick a category while entering a transaction and the budget appears** — what was budgeted, what has
  been used, and what remains.
- **It shows where this transaction lands you**, not just where you were: "$570 of $700 used — $700
  after this one," updating as you type the amount. No extra loading; the projection is calculated as
  you go.
- **Committed money counts.** Transactions entered but not yet cleared by the bank are included in what
  has been used, because money that is spent is spent whether or not it has cleared. Where pending
  money is involved the figure breaks it out, so it is clear what is counted. Note this is deliberately
  broader than the fiscal reports, which count only cleared transactions.
- **Choose a specific budget line and you see that line first**, with the whole category underneath —
  so you know both whether the line is covered and how the category is tracking.
- **Income reads as progress, not consumption**: "$15,490 of $15,490 expected — 100% received." Passing
  an income target is good news and is never flagged as a problem.
- **Back-dating works correctly.** Entering a June expense in August shows last fiscal year's budget,
  because the figures follow the transaction's date rather than today's.
- **A category with no budget says so plainly** rather than showing zeroes that could be mistaken for
  being at the limit.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `budget.view` *or* `ledger.manage` | Seeing budget figures while entering a transaction |

Recording transactions is gated separately; someone able to record but not to see budgets will simply
not see this panel.

#### New Routes

- `GET /api/admin/ledger/budget-context` — budget and spending for one fund and fiscal year

#### Known Limits

- The posted-and-pending breakdown appears only when there is pending money to break out.
- The reimbursement "mark paid" dialog does not show this context yet, though it uses the same budget
  line picker.
