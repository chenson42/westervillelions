## 1.37.0 — 2026-07-26

### Enhancement: Prospective Members

**Value:** People often engage with the club — visiting meetings, volunteering
— for a while before they formally join, and there's frequently a gap between
being inducted and actively participating. Until now the only way to get
someone onto the `club@westervillelions.org` email list was to add them as a
full member, which also billed them dues and gave them a portal account before
they were ready. Prospective folks who weren't added simply stopped hearing
from the club.

**Changes:**
- **New member status.** Every member now has a status of **Prospective**,
  **Active**, or **Ended** (replacing the old single "Active" checkbox on the
  member form). Prospective members receive the `club@` email list but are
  **not** billed dues, **not** counted in membership totals or exports, and get
  **no** member-portal login until they're inducted.
- **Smooth induction.** Changing a member from Prospective to Active
  automatically creates their portal account (with the welcome/password email)
  and records their join date — so nothing extra is needed at induction time.
- **"Approve as Prospective."** Membership-application review now has a third
  action alongside Approve and Reject: **Approve as Prospective** adds the
  applicant to the club email list without treating them as a full dues-paying
  member yet.
- **Directory badge.** Prospective members appear in the member directory with
  a gold **Prospective** badge, and the admin members list gains
  "Prospective only" / "Ended only" filters plus a per-row status indicator.
- **Fix:** Approving a membership application now immediately syncs the new
  member to the `club@` list (previously the sync only ran on manual member
  edits, so freshly approved members could miss emails until the next sync).

**Testing:**
- [x] Typecheck and full unit suite pass (21 new tests covering status-derived
  active state, provisioning gates, join-date resolution, and club-list
  eligibility)
- [ ] Live-database migration backfill spot-check (`is_active=false` rows →
  `membership_status='ended'`) — to run at deploy
- [ ] Manual click-through of the add-prospect, approve-as-prospective, and
  induction flows against a live environment
