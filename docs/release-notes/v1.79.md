## 1.79.0 — 2026-09-25

### Enhancement: The board's proposal notification is now complete and replyable

**Value:** When a member submits a project or activity proposal, the board already received an
email summarising it. That summary carried six of the proposal's fields — enough to know
something had been proposed, but not enough to discuss it without opening the site. Anyone who
hit "reply" reached the no-reply address rather than the member who submitted it.

**Changes:**

- **Every field the board sees on the review page is now in the email** — the five that were
  missing are estimated income, volunteers needed, what is needed from the club, the publicity
  plan, and any additional notes. The order matches the review page, so the email and the site
  read the same way.
- **Replying reaches the proposer.** A board member can now reply to the notification and have
  it go to the member who submitted the proposal, rather than to an unmonitored address.
- **"Not sure yet" and "left blank" still read differently.** The proposal form deliberately
  lets a member say they do not yet know a cost, a date, or a headcount, which is different from
  skipping the question. The email preserves that distinction on all four such fields rather
  than showing both as empty — so the board can tell an open question from an unanswered one.
- **Dates are readable.** The proposed date now reads "November 15, 2026" instead of
  "2026-11-15", matching how dates appear everywhere else in the club's email.

#### Known Limitation

If a notification fails to send and is retried from the email queue, the reply-to address is not
preserved on the retry, and a reply to that message would again reach the no-reply address. This
affects several of the club's emails, not just this one, and is tracked as backlog item **B-64**.
