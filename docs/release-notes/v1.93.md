## 1.93.0 — 2026-10-12

### Feature: A Visit to Santa's Workshop sign-up page

**Value:** A Visit to Santa's Workshop has its own page. Families can read the whole weekend plan at westervillelions.org/santas-workshop: bring a favorite stuffy to IHOP in Westerville on Friday, Dec 18, it has its portrait taken with Santa and Mrs. Claus on Saturday, and it comes home on Sunday, Dec 20, with a printed frame of its picture. Tickets are $15 per stuffy, with no limit on how many stuffies a family can send.

#### What's New

- Until Sunday, Nov 8 the page says when sign-ups open. While they are open it holds the ticket form, with a button to open the form in its own tab for phones that prefer it. After sign-ups close on Dec 16 it shows how the weekend works, and after the event it becomes a thank-you. The page changes state on its own, by the Eastern date, with no deploy.
- It says plainly how photos are handled (stuffies only, never the children, and a stuffy's portrait goes in the public collage only if the family says yes), how snack choices work, where the money goes, and how long photos and sign-up details are kept.
- The short address westervillelions.org/santasworkshop forwards to the page for the poster, and the old preview address forwards there too.
- The privacy policy now explains what the event collects and how long it is kept.
- Until the ticket form link is added, the page says the form is coming soon rather than showing a dead button.

#### Permissions

None. The page is public.

#### New Routes

- `GET /santas-workshop` — the sign-up page
- `GET /santasworkshop` — forwards to the page with tracking tags
- `GET /santas-workshop/preview/*` — forwards to the page (the preview prototype is retired)
