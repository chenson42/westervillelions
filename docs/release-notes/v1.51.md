## 1.51.0 — 2026-07-29

### Feature: Transfers & Sweeps — moving money the right way

**Value:** Two real treasury needs the Ledger couldn't handle before: moving cash between the club's
own bank accounts (e.g. funding Petty Cash from the operating account), and sweeping publicly-raised
money out of the club's Activity Fund to the Foundation where it belongs. Both are now first-class,
guard-railed actions.

#### What's New
- **Transfer** — move money between two of an entity's own bank accounts (each side now records the
  account it actually cleared, so each reconciles against its own bank statement). This is the
  supported way to **fund Petty Cash** (Administrative Checking → Petty Cash), which previously had no
  path and sat at $0.
- **Sweep to Foundation** — move publicly-raised money from the Club's **Activity Fund** to the
  **Foundation** as a single guided action that records the two correct entries on each set of books
  (Club expense + Foundation income). Requires a **board-minute reference** and lands the Foundation
  side as categorized charitable income (defaults to "Public donations," selectable). A confirmation
  step spells out that a sweep is two permanent entries on two separate books, not a reversible move.
- **Directional guardrails (deny-by-default).** The books now *enforce* the money-flow rules instead
  of only warning after the fact:
  - ✅ Club Activity → Foundation (the sweep)
  - ✅ Club account ↔ account (same fund, e.g. Admin Checking ↔ Petty Cash)
  - 🚫 Activity → Administrative (public money can't move into the operating fund)
  - 🚫 Foundation → Club, in any direction (charitable money is a one-way valve)
  - 🚫 everything else not explicitly sanctioned
- **Board approval on large moves.** Transfers and sweeps over the disbursement-approval threshold now
  route to the board Approvals queue like any other large expense — closing a prior gap where transfers
  posted immediately with no second signer. Small moves post directly. A pending transfer/sweep shows
  in Approvals as a single "From → To" line and approves both legs together.

#### Fixes shipped alongside
- The board-minute citation on a sweep is now **preserved** through board approval (the approver sees
  and keeps the original motion reference instead of being forced to overwrite it).
- Closed a data-trust gap: a transfer's entities are now derived from the funds themselves, never taken
  from the client.
- A transfer pair's per-leg bank accounts are now immutable after creation (a prior edit path could
  silently overwrite both legs with one account).

#### Permissions
| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `ledger.record` | Recording a transfer or sweep (unchanged) |
| `ledger.approve` | Approving/rejecting an over-threshold transfer or sweep (unchanged) |
