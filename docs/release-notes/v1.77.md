## 1.77.0 — 2026-09-21

### Feature: Reset a member's password from the admin screen

**Value:** When a member can't get into the portal, an admin no longer has to wait on email.
The member's page under Admin → Users now has a **Reset Password** button that sets a new
password immediately and shows it once, so you can read it to them over the phone or hand it
to them in person. Nothing is emailed, so it works regardless of whether the member ever
receives our mail.

#### What's New

- **A temporary password you can say out loud.** Twelve characters in `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` groups,
  drawn from an alphabet with the easily-confused characters removed — no capital `I` or `O`,
  no `0` or `1` — because this gets read over the phone.
- **Shown once, and it stays on screen until you dismiss it.** The password appears in a panel
  with a copy button and waits for you to click Done. It deliberately isn't a toast, which
  would vanish on its own and take the password with it.
- **Nothing else about the account changes.** Roles, event RSVPs, and everything the member has
  authored are untouched. This replaces a password; it does not rebuild an account.
- **Accounts that sign in with Google are handled honestly.** If the member has no password and
  signs in with Google, the button reads **Set Password** and the wording says plainly that
  this does not fix Google sign-in — it adds a second way in, alongside it.
- **You can't reset your own account this way.** Your own row points you at the normal
  forgot-password flow instead.

#### Known Limitation

Resetting a password does **not** sign out anyone already signed in on that account. Sessions
stay valid until they expire on their own. If the concern is that someone untrusted is signed
in right now, changing the password will not remove them.

### Fix: Donor linking now works on reconciled transactions

**Value:** The treasurer can attach a donor to a gift that has already been through a closed
bank reconciliation. Previously this failed with an error advising you to reopen the
reconciliation session — heavier and riskier advice than the task called for, since attaching
a donor changes no figure that the reconciliation depends on.

- **Only the donor link is allowed through.** A reconciled transaction still refuses any change
  to its amount, date, category, account, or check number — the request is rejected in full,
  not partially applied. The guarantee that a closed reconciliation's arithmetic cannot
  silently move is unchanged.
- **Unlinking works the same way**, and every such change is recorded with who made it and when.

### Fix: Officer titles now come from the Board of Directors roster

**Value:** The printable member directory and each member's own profile page were showing last
year's officers — the club President listed as 1st Vice President, the immediate past president
still listed as President, the sitting Treasurer listed as a first-year director. These now read
from the Board of Directors roster, which is kept current, so a title is correct the moment the
roster is.

- The **on-screen member directory was already correct** and is unchanged — it has always read
  from the Board roster.
- Only the printed roster and the profile page were affected.
