## 1.19.0 — 2026-06-24

### Feature: Annual Membership Dues Tracking

**Value:** The club can now track who has paid membership dues, on a Lions fiscal-year basis (Jul 1 – Jun 30), and quickly answer "who hasn't paid yet." Dues are recorded as a full payment log per member, with paid/partial/unpaid status derived automatically from what each member owes.

#### What's New

- **Dues admin** (`/admin/dues`): a per-fiscal-year list of members with their dues status (Paid / Partial / Unpaid), amount paid vs. expected, search, and a prominent **Unpaid** filter — the primary "who still owes" report.
- **Per-member detail**: a full payment log with add / edit / delete, supporting refunds (negative entries). Each payment records amount, method (Check / Cash / Zeffy / Other), date, and notes.
- **Quick "Mark Paid"**: a one-click action right in the dues list that records the member's remaining balance as an electronic (Zeffy) payment dated today — no need to open each member.
- **Family vs. individual rates**: each member has a dues category; family-rate members are billed the discounted family amount. FY2026 is seeded at **$120 individual / $96 family**, and dues amounts are configurable per fiscal year.
- **Active fiscal year**: an admin-set "active" year that all dues surfaces default to (set to FY2026), independent of the calendar date.
- **Member self-view** (`/members/dues`): each member can see their own dues status and payment history.
- **Members roster integration**: the admin members list gains a dues-status column and filter (visible only to users with dues access), so unpaid members can be surfaced from the standard roster.
- **CSV export** of dues status for a fiscal year, for the annual audit.
- **New Treasurer role**: treasurers record and manage dues. Chris Henson and James Shively are seeded as treasurers.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `dues.view` | View dues status, payment logs, and the members-list dues column (Admin, Board Member, Treasurer) |
| `dues.manage` | Record / edit / delete payments, configure amounts, set the active year and member category (Admin, Treasurer) |

Member self-view at `/members/dues` is gated to the signed-in member's own record (no feature required).

#### New Routes

- `GET /admin/dues`, `GET /admin/dues/[memberId]` — dues list and per-member detail
- `GET /members/dues` — member self-view
- `POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/admin/dues/[memberId]` (+ `/[paymentId]`, `/category`) — payment and category management
- `PATCH /api/admin/dues/settings` — configure per-year amounts and the active fiscal year
- `GET /api/admin/dues/export` — CSV export
- `GET /api/members/dues` — member self-view data
