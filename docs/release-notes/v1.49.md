## 1.49.0 — 2026-07-29

### Feature: Budget Star & Notes

**Value:** During budget prep and board meetings the treasurer needs to flag priority lines for
discussion and jot a working note ("confirm with youth committee," "matches last year + 5%") — right
on the budget, and carried onto the copy that gets mailed out for review.

#### What's New

- **Star (flag for discussion)** on any budget **category** or **beneficiary line item** — starred
  rows sort to the top of their section instantly, so you scan them first in a meeting.
- **Free-text note** (up to 500 characters) on the same rows — a small note icon opens an inline
  editor; filled rows show the note on the printable/mailed worksheet.
- Works on an **un-budgeted** category too (you can flag/annotate a line before you've set its
  amount — it won't show a fake $0).
- **Stays editable even after the budget is Approve-&-locked** — annotations are working notes, not
  the approved figures, so you can still leave "revisit next year" after locking.
- Shows **on screen and on the printable worksheet**, at both category and line-item grain.
- Admin-only — never shown on the member financial reports or impact pages.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `ledger.manage` | Star/note any budget line (unchanged — no new key) |

#### New Routes

- `PATCH /api/admin/ledger/budgets/annotations` — star/note a category (lazy-creates the row if needed)
- `PATCH /api/admin/ledger/budgets/cause-lines/annotations` — star/note a beneficiary line item

### Enhancement: Prior year in the budgeting fiscal-year picker

**Value:** The budgeting page's year picker only offered the current year forward, hiding a completed
prior-year budget you'd want to view as a reference.

**Changes:**
- The fiscal-year selector now offers **prior / current / next** (e.g. 2025 / 2026 / 2027) instead of
  current-and-forward, so last year's approved budget is selectable.
