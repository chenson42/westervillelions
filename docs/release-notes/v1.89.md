## 1.89.0 — 2026-10-10

A board member wrote in through the contact form: they could open The Ledger but had "no permission to
approve". They had the permission. On a phone, the Approve button was off the edge of the screen.

### Defect Fix: The Approvals page works on a phone

**Problem:** On a phone, and on any laptop narrower than about 1280 pixels, the Pending Approvals table
showed only the date, fund and amount. The Approve and Reject buttons were there, but off the right edge of
the screen with nothing to suggest the table scrolled sideways. A board member following the notification
email saw a pending disbursement and no way to act on it.

**Root Cause:** The table sat inside a card that scrolls horizontally with no visual cue. The admin sidebar
leaves too little room for the full table until the extra-large breakpoint, so the clipping affected laptops
as well as phones.

**Fix:** Below 1280 pixels each pending item is a card with large Approve and Reject buttons. The table
remains on wider screens.

**Testing:**
- [x] Board-only account (no admin, no treasurer) at 360, 390, 1024 and 1279 pixels sees cards with both buttons on screen; 1280 and 1440 see the table
- [x] Approve from the card posts the disbursement; Transfer and Sweep pairs post both legs or neither

### Enhancement: Approve and Reject are race-safe and say who decided

**Value:** Every board member is emailed when a disbursement needs approval, and only one approval is needed.
Before this release, two members acting at the same moment could both record an approval, and the second
person saw a generic error. Now exactly one decision lands.

**Changes:**
- If a colleague approves or rejects an item while you are looking at it, your window closes, the list
  refreshes, and a message says who acted. (A rejection says "a board member" for now; recording who
  rejected is coming next.)
- The Approve and Reject windows show the fund, the memo and who recorded the entry, so you can decide
  without going back to the list. Approve explains that it stays greyed out until a board-minute reference
  is entered.
- If you recorded an entry yourself, the page says another board member must decide it instead of offering
  buttons that would not work. The Reject button is hidden on those rows.
- The empty state now reads "Nothing is waiting for approval".
- Notification emails for pending disbursements display payee and memo text safely, whatever characters they
  contain.

### Security: Two transitive dependency advisories closed

**Background:** The pre-push CVE audit flagged two high advisories in packages pulled in by Next: `sharp`
(a librsvg issue, patched in 0.35.5) and `source-map-js` (event-loop denial of service, patched in 1.2.2).
The existing `sharp` override floored at 0.35.4, one release short.

**Changes:**
- The `sharp` override now requires 0.35.5 or later, and a new override requires `source-map-js` 1.2.2 or later.
- `pnpm audit --prod --audit-level=high` exits clean; the remaining one moderate and one low advisory are unchanged.

#### Coming next in this work

who already decided it, recording who rejected an entry, and the ability for more than one board member to
add their approval.
