## 1.66.0 — 2026-08-12

### Feature: Email a donor their acknowledgment letter

**Value:** The club has been able to produce a proper, IRS-compliant acknowledgment letter since
v1.61.0, but every one of them had to be printed and handed over or posted. Donor email addresses
were recorded at the time specifically for this. This is the last step.

#### What's New

- **Send a letter by email** from the same screen you already use to print them. Nothing about the
  letter changes: the same club wording, the same required legal paragraph, the same figures.
- **Print and email live side by side.** Some donors have an address on file and some do not, and
  the ones who do not are marked plainly so it is obvious who still needs a paper copy.
- **A donor with several addresses gets it at all of them.** The club's very first donor asked for
  exactly that, which is why more than one address can be held.
- **A letter can only be sent once.** If two people press send, or the same batch is submitted
  twice, the second attempt reports that it has already gone rather than sending it again. A donor
  will not receive the same receipt twice.
- **If a send fails outright, the letter is not marked as sent** — so it stays in the list to be
  dealt with, rather than looking finished when nothing arrived.
- **The record now says how a letter went out**, emailed or printed, so "who still needs a printed
  copy" has an answer.
- **The treasurer is copied on every one**, and replies come back to them.

#### An honest limitation, stated on screen

"Emailed" means the message was accepted for sending. It is **not** confirmation that the donor
received it: if it bounces afterwards, the club currently has no way of knowing. The screen says so
where you will see it. Making bounces visible is tracked as a follow-up, and until then the
treasurer's own copy of each letter is the practical check.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `ledger.record` | Emailing acknowledgment letters |

No new permission key, no new page.

## 1.65.0 — 2026-08-12

### Feature: Dues Reminder Emails

**Value:** Chasing unpaid dues meant working out who hadn't paid, then writing the same email over
and over, and remembering who had already been asked. This does the working-out, writes the note,
and keeps the record — while leaving the treasurer entirely in control of when it goes.

#### What's New

- **A warm note, not an invoice.** It opens by hoping the member is well, says the club's records
  don't show the dues yet, and offers "that happens on my end sometimes" as the likely explanation.
  No amount owed, no deadline, no implication anyone has been neglectful.
- **Nothing sends on its own.** No schedule, no automatic trigger. The treasurer picks the members
  and presses send, and the confirmation says exactly how many people will be emailed.
- **Members who have paid part of their dues are treated differently**, in a separate group that is
  switched off by default, with wording that never tells someone who has paid something that they
  haven't paid.
- **Members with no email address are shown, not quietly skipped**, so it is clear who still needs a
  word in person.
- **You can see who was last reminded and when**, with a warning before reminding anyone again
  within a fortnight.
- **Add a personal line** to any batch — the thing that makes it a note from a person rather than a
  mail-merge.
- **Signed by whoever holds the office of Treasurer**, taken from the board, not by whoever happens
  to press the button. So a second bookkeeper can send a letter correctly signed by the treasurer.
- Sent from **treasurer@westervillelions.org**, with replies and a copy going to the treasurer's own
  inbox, so "I paid in July" always reaches a person.
- The amounts quoted come from the club's dues settings, so the email can never disagree with the
  books.

### Enhancement: The treasurer is copied on club money emails

**Value:** The treasurer should never have to wonder whether a member was actually told something.

**Changes:**
- Reimbursement approved, rejected and paid notices, and both disbursement-approval notices, now
  copy the treasurer.
- The email log records who was copied and blind-copied, so a sent message is a faithful record of
  what actually went out.
- One shared definition of who the treasurer is, so the signature on a letter and the copy on a
  notice can never disagree.
- If the office is briefly unset, notices still go out without the copy rather than failing — a
  member must still learn their reimbursement was approved. A dues reminder, by contrast, refuses
  to send at all rather than go out signed by nobody.

### Security: Test emails can no longer reach real people

**Background:** In August two test runs sent genuine email to real club members. The first reached
the club's mailing list; the second sent sixteen board members a fake $500 approval request. The
protection at the time was a list of two addresses that were never to be written to, and neither
incident involved those addresses.

**Changes:**
- Outside the live site, the system now delivers to **nobody** unless an address has been
  deliberately allowed for development. Previously it delivered to everybody except two named
  addresses.
- This covers every email the club sends — reimbursements, approvals, minutes, proposals,
  suggestions, password resets — not only the newest feature.
- Blocked messages are still recorded and still show in the email log, so features can be tested
  end to end without anything leaving the building.
- The club's mailing lists are refused even if someone allows them by mistake.
