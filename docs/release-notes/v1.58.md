## 1.58.0 — 2026-08-07

### Feature: Lions International Membership Types

**Value:** Lions International classifies every member by type — Active, Life Member, Honorary, and so
on — and those types govern real things: who counts toward per-capita billing, who votes, and how the
club reports its roster upward. The club record had no way to say which type a member held, so that
information lived outside the system entirely. This puts it on the member record.

#### What's New

- **A "Lions International Membership Type" field** on each member record, offering all seven LCI
  types: Active, Member at Large, Honorary, Privileged, Life Member, Associate Member, and Affiliate
  Member.
- **An "LCI Type" column and filter on the members list**, so the whole roster can be reviewed at a
  glance and filtered down to, say, every Life Member — rather than opening records one at a time.
- Every existing member starts as **Active**, which is correct for the large majority. The handful of
  Life, Honorary, or Associate members can be corrected directly on their records, and those
  corrections are permanent — future updates to the site cannot reset them.

#### What This Does Not Change

- **Dues are unaffected.** Every active member continues to be billed local club dues regardless of
  type. The type is recorded for Lions International reporting; it does not drive billing, and no
  member's dues change as a result of this release.
- **The member directory and printed roster are unchanged.** Membership type is visible to
  administrators only — it does not appear in the directory or on the printed roster, which stays
  focused and readable.

#### A Note on Two Similar Fields

The member record now carries both **Membership Status** (whether someone is currently in the club:
prospective, active, or ended) and **Lions International Membership Type**. Both include a value
called "Active", but they mean different things — a Life Member is active in the club, and an Active
type member may have ended their membership. The two fields sit next to each other with distinct
labels and a note explaining the difference, and on the members list they are styled differently so
they cannot be mistaken for one another.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `members.edit` | Setting or changing a member's LCI membership type |

No new permission key. Anyone who can already edit member records can set the type.

### Enhancement: Member directory polish

**Value:** Small refinements to the directory shipped in v1.57.0, from using it.

**Changes:**
- The Print / Save as PDF control now sits inside the directory card, alongside the member count,
  rather than floating above it — and reads as a quiet text action rather than a heavy outlined
  button.
- Card corners across the member portal now match the rest of the site's styling, which had drifted.
