## 1.101.0 — 2026-11-04

### Feature: Santa's Workshop storyline scenes

**Value:** The group photos now tell a story. Before the weekend, the photo lead writes the storyline as a list of scenes, each with a title and a few lines of text. When volunteers stage a group photo they pick its scene on the phone, and the families' collage shows the pictures scene by scene, with each scene's title and text, in the order the storyline sets.

#### What's New

- **Scenes card** on each session's page: add, edit, reorder and delete scenes (title up to 60 characters, text up to 280, up to 20 scenes). Deleting a scene never deletes a photo; its photos move to "Other pictures". The card reminds the writer that scene text is public and must not name children, families or stuffies; text that looks like an email, a web link or a phone number is refused.
- **Volunteer phones:** the group-photo screen asks for the scene first (or "Other"). The choice stays selected between photos, the preview shows "For: <scene>", and it works without signal; a photo sent for a scene that was deleted in the meantime still uploads, under "Other pictures".
- **Fix a wrong tap:** on the session's Photos page, each group photo shows its scene and a "Move to scene" control.
- **The collage reads as a story:** scenes in order with their title and text, then "Other pictures", then the stuffies' portraits. A scene shows only once it has a published picture. A session without scenes looks exactly as before. Who can see which photos is unchanged.

#### Before the event

- Check on a real phone before the event: photos taken with no signal, a scene deleted while photos wait to send, and "Choose a saved photo" on an iPhone.
- Before publishing the real collage on Dec 19, read every scene title and text on the live page: the site cannot tell when a name has been typed.
- Clone session does not copy scenes yet; retype them into the real session, or wait for the copy button (before Dec 14).

#### Database

- New table `sw_scenes` and a nullable `sw_photos.scene_id` (migration 0117). Additive; applied automatically on deploy.
