## 1.96.0 — 2026-10-13

### Feature: Santa's Workshop photo collage

**Value:** Santa's Workshop now has a public photo collage. After the event, an admin reviews the group pictures, hides any that should not be shared, and publishes the collage; the link on each family's printed frame then opens a quiet page of group pictures and the portraits of stuffies whose families agreed to share.

#### What's New

- The page never shows a name, and families who did not agree to share are kept off it.
- A photo can be hidden at any time and disappears right away. The admin can unpublish the whole page, or publish pictures taken after the first publish in a second step; portraits retaken after publishing wait for that step too, so nothing goes public by accident.
- Before the photos are published, the same link simply says when to check back. After the club takes the photos down, it says so.
- The admin photos page shows which pictures a stranger can see, and gives the collage link and a QR code for the printed frames.
- The public page says the club photographs stuffies only, never children, and gives the club's email address for anyone who wants a picture taken down.

#### Permissions

- None new. Reviewing and publishing use the existing Santa's Workshop permission.

#### New Routes

- `GET /santas-workshop/c/<token>` — the public collage (anyone with the link)
- `GET /admin/santas-workshop/<session>/photos` — review, hide and publish (admin)
