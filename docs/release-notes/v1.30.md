## 1.30.0 — 2026-07-21

### Feature: Impact Cause Drill-Down

**Value:** Members could see cause totals on the Our Community Impact page but
had no way to learn what actually made up a number. Transparency into the
club's giving is the whole point of the dashboard — now every total can be
opened up.

#### What's New

- On `/members/impact`, each row in "Giving by Cause" is now a tappable,
  keyboard-operable control that expands inline to list every individual gift
  behind that cause's total — date, recipient, and amount.
- The expanded list always reconciles: gift amounts sum exactly to the cause
  total shown on the bar, including gifts with no recorded recipient (shown as
  "Recipient not recorded").
- Expansion follows you across fiscal-year pills — switch from FY2025–26 to
  All-time and an open cause stays open, showing the wider scope.
- Fully accessible: real buttons with focus rings and `aria-expanded`, and the
  layout holds up on a phone even with long gift lists.

#### Permissions

No new keys — the page's existing two-tier gate (`impact.view` when visibility
is board-only; any linked member when club-wide) covers the new detail.

### Feature: Failed-Login Visibility

**Value:** The club had zero visibility into failed sign-in attempts — a
brute-force run against a member's password, a member locked out and quietly
giving up, or a deactivated user trying to get back in were all invisible.
Now the admin can see them.

#### What's New

- New **Admin → Security** page listing every failed sign-in attempt: when it
  happened, the email tried, which provider (password or Google), and why it
  failed — six distinct failure reasons are distinguished, including
  deactivated-account attempts via Google OAuth.
- Two views: a searchable reverse-chronological log, and a grouped per-email
  view (failure counts + last attempt) for spotting brute-force patterns at a
  glance.
- Records are kept 90 days and pruned automatically — no maintenance needed.
- The public sign-in experience is completely unchanged, and the error message
  stays identical for every failure type, so attackers can't probe which
  emails have accounts.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `admin.security_view` | Viewing the Security page (bound to Admin only) |

### Feature: Structured Check Numbers (The Ledger)

**Value:** Check numbers lived only in the original Quicken registers — the
Ledger had no field for them, which blocked the upcoming bank-reconciliation
feature (check number is the most reliable way to match a book entry to a
bank-statement line) and left the uncashed-checks list unable to say *which*
check is outstanding.

#### What's New

- Transactions now carry a **Check #** field — visible in the transaction form
  (create and edit, hidden for transfers) and as a new column in the
  dashboard's uncashed-checks list.
- **101 historical checks got their numbers back**, recovered from the
  original register exports with a match-don't-guess backfill (0 failed
  matches; 4 same-day twin checks deliberately left for manual entry).
- Three transactions mistagged as checks were corrected to debit-card, with
  treasurer approval, after the register showed they were card purchases.

### Security: Dependency CVE Fix

**Background:** The monthly-audit gate caught a high-severity DoS advisory in
a transitive dependency (`brace-expansion`, reached via the spreadsheet-export
library).

**Changes:**
- Forced the affected version range up to the patched release via a scoped
  package override; full test suite and build verified after the bump.

---

### Infrastructure: Receipt-Upload Groundwork

**Background:** The next Ledger feature — attaching receipt photos to expense
transactions, with a waiver path for historical rows — has its database
groundwork in this release. It is inert until the upload and viewing surfaces
ship.

**Changes:**
- The unused paste-a-URL receipt column was migrated to the private
  storage-key pattern used by reimbursement receipts, and waiver audit fields
  (who, when, why) were added.
- The missing-receipt compliance count now excludes waived rows, preparing
  for the waiver workflow.
