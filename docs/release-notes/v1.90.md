## 1.90.0 — 2026-10-10

v1.89 made the Approvals page usable on a phone. This release finishes the work it promised: you can see what the
board has already decided, follow the email straight to the entry, and add your own approval after a colleague has
posted it.

### Feature: Add your approval

**Value:** When a disbursement, transfer or sweep needs a board decision, the first approval still posts it, exactly
as before. Now other board members can add their approval afterward, so the record shows who on the board agrees.
Adding your approval is a record only: it does not change the books, and it never holds anything up.

#### What's New

- On the Approvals page, each entry in "Recently decided" shows "Also approved by" with each board member's name and
  date, and an "Add your approval" button on any entry you did not record, approve or already sign.
- The link in the approval email shows the same thing: who approved, who else has added their approval, and a button
  to add yours.
- If you press Approve a moment after a colleague, your window closes, a message says who approved first, and you land
  on the entry where you can add your approval.
- Your name is added once per entry. For a transfer or sweep, one approval covers both sides.
- There is no required number of approvals. The first approval posts the entry; added approvals are a record of
  agreement.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `ledger.approve` | The Approvals page, Approve, Reject and Add your approval (the new co-sign route uses the same key; no new permission) |

#### New Routes

- `POST /api/admin/ledger/transactions/[id]/cosign` — add your approval to an entry that has already posted
- `GET /admin/ledger/approvals?tab=decided` — the Recently decided tab
- `GET /admin/ledger/approvals?txn=<id>` — opens the Approvals page on one entry, highlighted

### Enhancement: See what the board has decided, and follow the email to the exact entry

**Value:** A board member who opens the approval email after a colleague has acted used to see nothing that explained
what had happened. Now the page says who decided and when, and the whole recent history is one tab away.

**Changes:**
- A new "Recently decided" tab lists the last 25 approvals and rejections, newest first, with the amount, payee, fund,
  who recorded the entry, who decided it, and the full board-minute reference or rejection reason.
- The link in the approval email opens the exact disbursement, transfer or sweep. If it is still waiting, it is
  highlighted; if a colleague already decided it, a note says who and when. This also works when you have to sign in
  first.
- Rejections now record who rejected the entry and when, from now on. (Rejections made before this release show
  "a board member".)
- The notification email tells recipients what to expect when a colleague has already approved.

### Defect Fix: Approvals wording and figures

**Problem:** Messages and buttons on the Approvals page talked about "disbursements" even when the entry was a
transfer or a sweep, a sweep did not say which book (Club or Foundation) each side belonged to, and dollar amounts
over a thousand had no thousands separator.

**Root Cause:** The first version of the Approve and Reject windows used one generic noun and a plain number format
for every kind of entry.

**Fix:** Transfers and sweeps are named as such on their buttons and messages, sweeps show the fund and book on each
side, and amounts show thousands separators.

**Testing:**
- [x] A transfer and a sweep each read as a transfer or sweep in the Approve and Reject windows
- [x] A four-figure amount reads with a comma on the card, the dialog and the Recently decided list

### Security: Sign-in links stay on this site

**Background:** The approval email link now carries the entry through sign-in, so the sign-in and registration pages
decide where to send you afterward.

**Changes:**
- Sign-in and registration links now refuse to send anyone to an address outside the club's site.
