## 1.99.0 — 2026-10-17

This is the first of two steps toward an itemized sweep, where each online gift is copied into the Foundation
with its donor, date and category instead of arriving as one lump amount. This step prepares the books for it.
**Sweeps themselves work exactly as before and still record one lump amount.** The itemized sweep is the next
release.

### Feature: Public Funds, Foundation counterparts and donors on every gift

**Value:** The fund that holds the club's online and event money now goes by the name the club chose, Public
Funds, on every Ledger screen, message and page of the Treasury guide. A Public Funds category can say which
Foundation category it belongs with, so the books are ready for the itemized sweep. And the treasurer can attach
the donor while keying a gift instead of afterward.

#### Do this first: rename the fund

Nothing renames the fund for you. After this update:

1. Open the Public Funds register at **Ledger, Activity** (`/admin/ledger/activity`).
2. Choose **Edit fund**.
3. Change "Activity Fund" to "Public Funds" and save.

Every screen, warning, refusal message and the Treasury guide then follows the name you set; the edit is recorded
in the audit log. Until you rename it, everything keeps saying "Activity Fund", except a small "Public" tag on the
budgeting pages that appears right away and matches once the name is changed. Only a person who can manage the
Ledger can rename the fund.

#### What's New

- **Every screen follows the fund's name.** Transfer and sweep refusals, the warnings in the Move and Delete
  windows, the guardrail messages, the reports, the approvals pages and the Treasury guide all read the name
  stored on the fund, so a later rename changes them all at once.
- **Foundation counterparts for Public Funds categories.** On **Settings, Categories**, each Public Funds income
  category has a **Counterpart** action that picks the Foundation category it belongs with. Two are set for you:
  **Public donations** and **White Cane**. You can clear or change a counterpart at any time, and merging or
  deactivating a category tells you what points to it.
  **Today's sweep does not use the counterpart yet.** It still records one lump amount, and no copy of a gift or
  its donor reaches the Foundation. The counterpart is a record of intent until the itemized sweep ships.
- **Pick or create the donor while keying a gift.** On new Public Funds income and new Foundation income, the
  gift form has a donor field. You can search for a donor or add a new one without leaving the form.
- **The gift form tells you where the gift belongs.** Under the category it says whether the gift is Foundation-bound
  and whether a written receipt will be needed (gifts of $250 or more, unless the Foundation category says no
  receipt is required). A category with no counterpart says it is recorded as staying in Public Funds. These notes
  describe where the gift is meant to go; today's lump sweep does not check them.
- **A donor's page totals Foundation gifts only.** Total Giving no longer counts a gift that sits in Public Funds,
  and such a row is labeled "not yet a Foundation gift". A donor whose gift was moved into Public Funds will show a
  lower total than before, and a donor with only Public Funds gifts shows $0.00.
- **Counterpart categories read their flags from the Foundation.** "Counts as giving" and "Receipt not required"
  on a category with a counterpart show the Foundation category's values and cannot be edited on the Public Funds
  side. To change them there, clear the counterpart first. The Foundation's Form 990 line is shown for information.

#### What the treasurer should do

- **Reactivate the Public Funds categories you use before keying a gift.** Every Public Funds category is
  currently inactive in the books (they were retired in the August cleanup of unused categories), and the gift form
  can only pick active ones. This is how things stood before this release, not something it caused. On
  **Settings, Categories**, turn on **Show inactive** for Public Funds and reactivate the categories you use. The
  two counterparts that were set for you show there only with that switch on.
- **Choose a counterpart for Zeffy Donations when you reactivate it.** It has none today. The same question is open
  for any other Public Funds category whose gifts are meant for the Foundation (Rudolph Run, Pancake Breakfast,
  Sponsorships and Interest are the likely ones); the itemized sweep will leave a category with no counterpart in
  Public Funds.
- **Give the Foundation's White Cane category a Form 990 line.** It has none, and the category screen shows "No Form
  990 line" beside it as a reminder.

#### Permissions

- None new. Setting or clearing a counterpart uses **Manage Ledger**; choosing a donor on a gift uses the same
  permission as recording the gift.
