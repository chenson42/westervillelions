## 1.42.1 — 2026-07-28

### Defect Fix: Outstanding Checks No Longer Hide Later Statements

**Problem:** On the new member Financial Statements page, the Foundation's statements
stopped at February 2026 — March, April, May, and June were missing — and both funds
were harder to bring current than expected. A month only appears once it's reconciled,
but the check for "is this month reconciled?" was counting **outstanding checks** (checks
written and sent but not yet cashed by the recipient) as unreconciled activity. Because
an uncashed check blocks its own month *and every month after it*, two Foundation checks
written in early March hid four months of otherwise-final statements.

**Root Cause:** The reconciliation gate flagged any posted, unreconciled transaction on
or before a month's end — without excluding outstanding checks, which are a normal
reconciling item, not a sign the month is still open. (The statement itself already
handles them correctly, showing the book balance and footnoting uncashed checks.)

**Fix:** The gate now ignores outstanding checks — specifically expense payments made by
check that haven't cleared — when deciding whether a month is ready, in both places that
computed it. Genuinely unreconciled activity still hides a month: a deposit that hasn't
cleared, a debit-card charge, or **dues paid by paper check** (which are income, not an
outstanding check) all correctly keep a month gated until reconciled.

**Result:** The Foundation's statements now show through the latest fully-elapsed month
(June 2026), with uncashed checks footnoted as before. The Club's June still waits on its
real June activity being reconciled — as it should.

**Testing:**
- [x] Typecheck, production build, and full suite pass (614 tests, +3 for this fix)
- [x] Reproduced fail-without / pass-with, including the key guard that dues paid by
  paper check still gate a month (the fix keys on expense-checks only, not all checks)
- [x] Verified against live data: the Foundation fund with its two real outstanding
  March checks now resolves to June, not February

## 1.42.0 — 2026-07-28

Brings the treasurer's monthly board report into the member portal, so the whole
club can see where the money stands — without printing a page.

### Feature: Monthly Statement of Financial Condition

**Value:** The club's financial accountability lived in a monthly PDF the treasurer
produced and printed. This puts that same statement in front of every member, on
their own schedule, and lets anyone save it as a PDF — reproducing the format the
previous treasurer used, straight from the live books.

#### What's New

- **A new member-portal page** (`/members/financial-reports`) where any signed-in
  member can pick an entity (Club or Foundation) and a month and read that month's
  **Statement of Financial Condition** — the familiar three-column layout: **One
  Month**, **Twelve Months** (fiscal year to date), and **Annual Budget**, split into
  revenue and expense lines with net income and beginning/ending fund balance.
- **Save as PDF / print** — the page is print-styled, so the browser's "Save as PDF"
  produces a clean statement with the club chrome stripped out. No paper required.
- **Auto-published once a month is reconciled.** A month's statement only appears
  after every posted transaction on or before its last day has been reconciled — so
  members never see half-closed numbers. There's no extra "publish" step; it appears
  the moment the books are closed for that month.
- **Two funds, matching what's always been published** — the Club's Administrative
  fund and the Foundation's Charitable fund. (Internal pass-through and scholarship
  funds are not exposed.)
- **Book balance, honestly footnoted.** The statement shows the ledger's book balance,
  with a footnote when it differs from cash-in-bank (typically outstanding checks that
  haven't cleared) — the standard, correct way these two figures diverge at month-end.

#### Permissions

| Requirement | For |
|-------------|-----|
| Any linked member (signed in) | Viewing the statements — no separate permission key |

Viewing is open to the whole membership by design; the numbers shown are fund-level
totals only — no individual transactions, payees, donors, or member data are exposed.

#### New Routes

- `GET /members/financial-reports` — entity + month picker (lists only reconciled, ready months)
- `GET /members/financial-reports/[entity]/[month]` — a single month's statement (print-friendly)
