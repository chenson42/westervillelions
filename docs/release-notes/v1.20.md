## 1.20.0 — 2026-06-24

### Feature: The Ledger — Increment 1 ("Books")

**Value:** The first increment of an online back-office accounting system for the club's two entities — the **Westerville Lions Club** (501(c)(4)) and the **Westerville Lions Foundation** (501(c)(3) giving arm). Treasurers can keep the books online — record income, expenses, and inter-fund transfers against the Lions two-fund structure — and the board can see balances and budget-vs-actual at a glance. Grounded in the Lions Financial Transparency procedures and IRS guidance. This is the recording/reporting foundation; later increments add approvals + the fund firewall, the federal-filing compliance calendar, a member philanthropy dashboard, and donor acknowledgments.

#### What's New

- **`/admin/ledger`** — switch between the Club and the Foundation; per-entity overview showing each fund's balance, gross receipts, and a panel of compliance guardrails (negative fund, high reserves, treasurer bonding, un-itemized income, cash disbursements, missing receipts).
- **Funds** — Club: Administrative + Activity; Foundation: Charitable + Scholarship. Opening balances are editable in-app.
- **Transactions** — record / edit / delete income and expenses, with a category catalogue seeded from the Lions transparency doc. **Transfers** between funds are recorded as a linked debit + credit pair (atomic); editing or deleting one side updates/removes both.
- **Budgets & reports** — set an annual budget per fund/category and read a **Budget / Actual / Variance** fund report for any fiscal year (unbudgeted lines show "—").
- **Fiscal year** — Lions Jul 1 – Jun 30, labeled by its starting year, shared with the dues feature so the two always agree.

**Note:** entity EINs, bank names, and opening balances ship as placeholders and are editable in the admin UI under the manage permission — set the real values there.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `ledger.view` | View the ledger, fund reports, and overview (Admin, Treasurer, Board Member — read-only) |
| `ledger.record` | Record / edit / delete transactions (Admin, Treasurer) |
| `ledger.manage` | Edit funds, opening balances, and budgets (Admin) |

Board members have read-only access; regular members have none.

#### New Routes

- `GET /admin/ledger`, `GET /admin/ledger/[fundSlug]`, `GET /admin/ledger/[fundSlug]/report`
- `POST /api/admin/ledger/transactions` (income/expense and the atomic two-row transfer)
- `PATCH·DELETE /api/admin/ledger/transactions/[id]`
- `PATCH /api/admin/ledger/budgets` · `PATCH /api/admin/ledger/funds/[id]`
