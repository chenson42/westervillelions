## 1.72.0 — 2026-09-04

### Feature: Event Announcement Emails

**Value:** The season-kickoff invite went out through Zeffy and a third of the club never saw
it — marketing-platform mail from an unfamiliar sending domain lands in spam and promotions
tabs, especially for members on older email providers. Meeting notices need to reach everyone,
and the club had no way to send one from its own trusted address. Now it does.

#### What's New

- **Announce any event to the whole club** — admins and board members get an "Announce" button
  on each event in the admin Events area. It emails every active member from the club's own
  westervillelions.org address (the same trusted path password-reset and dues emails already
  use), with the event's details and the calendar file attached.
- **Occurrence or whole series** — announce a single date ("meeting this Thursday") or an
  entire recurring series ("we're back — here's the season"). A series announcement attaches
  the full-season calendar, so one tap puts every meeting in a member's calendar with
  automatic reminders from then on.
- **See exactly who won't get it** — before sending, the recipient preview shows every active
  member, with members who have no email address on file called out in their own section
  rather than silently skipped, so they can be reached another way.
- **Optional personal note** — the email body is built automatically from the event's details
  and signed "Westerville Lions Club"; the sender can add a short note on top, so every
  announcement is consistent and nothing critical gets forgotten.
- **A permanent record** — every send is logged per member: who was emailed, when, by whom,
  and whether each individual send succeeded. Each event shows its announcement history. The
  record says "Emailed," never "Delivered" — the club can prove what it sent, and the system
  doesn't overclaim what it can't see.
- **Retried emails keep their calendar attachment** — re-sending a queued announcement from
  the email-queue screen preserves the attached calendar file instead of silently dropping it.

#### Permissions

| Feature key | Required for |
|-------------|--------------|
| `events.announce` | Seeing the Announce button and sending announcements (granted to Admin and Board Member roles) |

Sending announcements is deliberately a narrower power than editing events — being able to
update an event's details doesn't automatically grant the ability to email the entire club.

#### New Routes

- `/admin/events/[id]/announce` — recipient preview, scope picker, note, send, and history
