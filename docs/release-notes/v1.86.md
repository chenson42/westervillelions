## 1.86.0 — 2026-10-01

Two of the changes in this release change how the treasurer corrects mistakes (Move and Delete), so those
sections end with what to keep in mind. The other two close gaps in how settled entries are protected.

### Feature: Move an entry to another fund

**Value:** When a gift is booked to the wrong fund, the treasurer can now correct it in place instead of
deleting and re-entering it. The original entry keeps its date, party, amount, bank account and
reconciliation status; only the fund changes, and the change is logged with who made it and why.

#### What's New

- **Move to another fund.** On an Administrative Fund income entry, choose **Move**, pick the Activity
  Fund, choose a category (Public donations is preselected), and give a reason of at least 10
  characters. Before you confirm, the dialog shows what will change: the Administrative and Activity
  balances go down and up by the amount, and the bank account balance is unchanged, because the cash
  never moved.
- **Then sweep.** After a move, the success screen offers **Record sweep now**, which opens the Sweep to
  Foundation form with the amount and the bank account filled in. The board-minute reference is still
  yours to enter, and moving an entry does not sweep anything by itself.
- **Settled entries can be moved too.** An entry that has already been reconciled, or that is dated in an
  earlier fiscal year, can also be moved. Those moves need the Manage Ledger permission (held by
  administrators by default). Reconciliation is not reopened and no closed month's figures change,
  because reconciliation is tied to the bank account and not to the fund.
- **Every move is on the record.** The Compliance page has a new **Recent corrections** section listing
  every move and delete from the last 90 days: who, when, the amount, from and to, and the reason.
  Entries that were already settled are marked so.

#### What the treasurer should know

- **Move goes one way, and only for one case.** This release moves income from the Administrative Fund
  to the Activity Fund, inside the same entity (the Club). It cannot move an entry back, cannot move an
  expense, and cannot move an entry between the Club and the Foundation. Money in the Activity Fund
  leaves only through a minuted sweep, so if you move the wrong entry, delete it and enter it again.
  If it was already reconciled, reopen its reconciliation session first.
- **Entries that are approved, rejected, pending, part of a transfer, or posted from dues cannot be
  moved.** The register now says so on the row (see below).
- **A move does not change which months show on the members' financial reports.** A month still
  appears there only once everything in it is reconciled.
- **If the month's Administrative statement was already sent to the board,** it will read as changed and
  you will be offered a corrected resend. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **An old gift moved to the Activity Fund can raise the holding-period warning right away,** because it
  keeps its original date. That is correct. Sweeping it clears the warning.
- **The system did not flag the original mistake and still will not.** It cannot yet tell that a
  donation entered under Administrative is public money. That check is on the backlog.
- **A gift that was physically deposited in the Club's bank account but belongs to the Foundation** is
  not a Move. Delete that entry, enter it again as Activity Fund income on Administrative Checking, then
  record the sweep to the Foundation. If a receipt letter was already sent for the original entry,
  Delete will refuse (see below) and the correction is a refund entry instead.
- Gift acknowledgment letters are unchanged. The Foundation side of a sweep is a transfer with no donor
  attached, so no acknowledgment letter is generated for it.

### Feature: Delete asks why, and keeps a record

**Value:** Delete used to remove an entry without a trace, and it could silently destroy a gift's receipt
letter along with it. It now requires a reason, records what was deleted, and refuses to delete a gift
whose receipt letter has already gone to the donor.

#### What's Changed

- **A reason is required** (10 to 500 characters). The dialog lists what else is removed with the entry.
- **A record of every delete** is kept, including the date, party, amount, fund, bank account, category,
  memo and reconciliation state, and appears in **Recent corrections** on the Compliance page. A deleted
  transfer or sweep (two linked entries) appears as one record.
- **A gift with a sent acknowledgment letter cannot be deleted.** The dialog says a receipt was already
  sent to the donor and to record a refund entry instead. A letter that was prepared but not sent is
  removed with the entry, and the dialog says so before you confirm.
- **Locked entries explain themselves.** Approved, rejected and reconciled entries now show a short label
  on the row saying why, with Edit and Delete greyed out and the next step as hover text on small
  screens and in the row on larger ones. Transfer and dues-posted entries are labelled too. Approved
  entries are still corrected with a refund entry, and reconciled entries by reopening the session.
- Approved, rejected and closed-session entries still cannot be deleted, as before.

### Fix: The reconcile checkbox could unlock a row cleared by a closed reconciliation

**Value:** The per-row reconcile checkbox in the register used to be able to un-reconcile an entry that a
closed reconciliation session had cleared, which silently broke that session's totals and unlocked the
entry for editing and deleting.

#### What's Changed

- An entry cleared by a closed reconciliation session now shows its checkbox greyed out, with an
  explanation, and the server refuses the change in either direction. To change such an entry, reopen
  the reconciliation session, which requires the Manage Ledger permission.
- Entries marked reconciled with the older per-row checkbox, and never closed in a session, still toggle
  as before.

---

### Fix: Bank accounts are checked when you enter or edit a transaction

**Value:** The bank account on a new or edited entry was never verified, so an account belonging to the
wrong organization could be saved, and a mistyped one produced a server error instead of a message.

#### What's Changed

- The bank account must exist, belong to the same entity as the entry, and be active. Otherwise you get
  a plain message and nothing is saved.
- Changing the bank account of an entry that is matched to a bank line in an open reconciliation session
  is refused. Unmatch it from the session first.
- Editing an entry without changing its bank account is unaffected.
- Sending a fund change through the ordinary Edit form now returns a clear message pointing to Move to
  another fund, instead of appearing to succeed and changing nothing.
