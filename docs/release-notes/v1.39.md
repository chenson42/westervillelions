## 1.39.0 — 2026-07-27

This release rounds out the budget calculator with the ability to shape a
budget line by line and formally lock it in for the year, and adds in-app
guidance explaining how Lions-club budgeting works.

### Enhancement: Add / Remove Budget Lines & Approve/Lock the Year

**Value:** You could seed and adjust a budget, but you couldn't add a brand-new
line for something you hadn't budgeted before, and there was no way to "lock it
in" once the board adopted it. Both are now built.

**Changes:**
- **Add a line.** On the Budgeting page you can now add a budget line for a
  category — including **creating a brand-new category inline** (name, income or
  expense, counts-toward-giving) right where you're budgeting. A newly created
  category is a real ledger category, so when you later record spending it lands
  in the same bucket and your budget-vs-actual numbers line up. (This also fixes
  a gap where a brand-new fund with no categories had no way to get its first
  line.)
- **Remove a line.** An explicit remove control drops a category's target for
  the year. It removes only the *budget target* — the category and any actual
  transactions recorded against it are untouched. Removing a line with an amount
  entered asks for confirmation first.
- **Approve & lock for the year.** When the board adopts the budget, an approver
  can **lock it** for an entity and fiscal year, recording who approved it, the
  date, and a board-minute reference. A locked budget becomes read-only — no
  edits, no re-seeding, no new lines — enforced on the server, not just hidden
  in the page.
- **Unlock / amend.** An approver can unlock a locked budget to make changes,
  with a required free-text reason recorded on the unlock.
- **Two levels of access.** Building and editing a budget uses the existing
  "manage ledger" permission; approving/locking and unlocking use the "approve"
  permission (the same one used for approving transactions and reimbursements),
  so a board member can adopt a budget without needing full edit rights.
- Before you lock, each fund's balance status is shown as a final check — but
  it never blocks you, since a planned drawdown on a charitable fund is
  legitimate.

### Enhancement: In-App Budgeting Guide

**Value:** The rules that make Lions-club budgeting different — two
self-balancing funds, public money that can't fund operations — now live
inside the app instead of a separate document.

**Changes:**
- A new **Budgeting** section in the Treasury User's Guide covers the two-fund
  rule (with its governing citation), how our Club and Foundation funds map,
  building a budget from prior-year actuals, why the admin side holds a reserve
  while the charitable side is meant to be disbursed, donor tax-deductibility,
  and a cross-link to the compliance calendar.
- The Budgeting page links straight to it ("How budgeting works →"), and each
  fund's balance indicator now carries a one-line explanation of why the rule
  differs by fund.
