## 1.50.0 — 2026-07-29

### Feature: Budget permissions & Budget Committee role

**Value:** Until now, editing a budget was possible only for full admins — so a treasurer who
wasn't also an admin could open the budgeting page and lock/unlock a budget but couldn't change a
single line. Budgeting now has its own view/edit permissions, plus a dedicated role for the people
who actually build the budget.

#### What's New
- Two new permission capabilities: **Budget: View** and **Budget: Edit**, separate from the general
  Ledger management permission.
- A new **Budget Committee** role that can view and edit budgets (including beneficiary cause-line
  breakdowns) and see the Ledger for context — but does **not** adopt/lock the budget.
- **Adopting/locking a budget stays with the board** (unchanged) — building the budget and approving
  it remain deliberately separate.
- Treasurers now get budget view + edit directly, and board members get read access — nobody who
  could reach budgeting before loses access.
- The admin sidebar's **Budgeting** link is now visible to anyone with any form of budget access
  (previously it was hidden unless you held full Ledger management).

### Feature: Split a transaction

**Value:** When one recorded charge actually cleared the bank as two separate lines (e.g. an event
platform payout landing in two deposits), it couldn't be matched during reconciliation. You can now
split it into parts that each match their own bank line.

#### What's New
- A **Split** action on an unreconciled transaction in the fund register opens a small dialog; enter
  an amount and it creates a second row for that amount and reduces the original by the same — the
  two always sum to the original.
- Repeatable (split a part again), and each part inherits the original's category, party, memo,
  payment method, check number, date, and bank account.
- **Guardrails** prevent splitting anything that would corrupt the books: approved, rejected, or
  reconciled transactions, a transaction already matched to a bank line in an open reconciliation
  session, or a leg of a transfer pair — each blocked with a plain-language message.

### Fix: Every transaction carries a bank account

**Value:** Transactions created with no bank account were invisible to reconciliation and could
silently go unmatched. Now every transaction has one by construction.

**Changes:**
- Recording a dues payment now automatically stamps the club's operating bank account on the ledger
  entry it creates.
- The manual transaction form **pre-fills each entity's default (operating) account** and now
  **requires** a bank account — the old blank "Not specified" option is gone. Editing an older entry
  that was missing an account pre-fills the default so it's a one-click fix.
- Each entity has one designated default account (Administrative Checking for the Club, Foundation
  Checking for the Foundation); Petty Cash stays a deliberate manual choice.

### Fix: Admin nav & membership page polish

- The **Membership** admin menu item is now labeled **Applications**.
- On the membership applications page, the action buttons (Approve / Approve as Prospective / Reject)
  no longer overflow their column on desktop — they wrap/stack cleanly instead of the red Reject
  button pushing outside its box.
