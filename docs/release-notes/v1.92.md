## 1.92.1 — 2026-10-12

### Infrastructure: dev-only eval allowance in the content-security policy

**Background:** Under `pnpm dev`, React's debugging tools call `eval()` and the site's policy forbids it, so every local page showed a console error. Production React never uses `eval()`.

**Changes:**
- The policy allows `eval()` only when the server runs in development mode. The production header is unchanged.

## 1.92.0 — 2026-10-11

### Feature: White Cane 2026 donation page

**Value:** The club's White Cane fundraiser runs all electronic this year: a poster with a QR code sends people to a page that tells the story of the white cane and the Lions, explains who is asking, and takes the gift online through Zeffy. No buckets, no cash handling, and every scan is counted.

#### What's New

- A new page at `/white-cane` tells the story of the white cane and the Lions (Peoria 1930, Lions International 1931, Ohio's yield law, White Cane Safety Day on October 15), explains who is asking, and shows the Zeffy donation form right on the page so a visitor can give without another tap. A plain "open the form in a new tab" link sits beside it for anyone the embedded form does not suit.
- The short address westervillelions.org/whitecane forwards to the page and tags poster scans so the club can count them.
- Gifts are received by the Westerville Lions Club Foundation, the same 501(c)(3) account as the donate page, and the page says so with the EIN. What the gift funds is filled in once the board answers; until then the page says gifts support the Lions' sight projects.
- The page is built to be reused every year: it closes itself on November 1 with a thank-you and opens again in mid-September, so next year's campaign needs only a new form link.
- The homepage hero's gold button points to the White Cane page while an active homepage announcement links to it, so the club turns the button on and off from Announcements, with dates, no deploy needed.
- A print-ready 22 by 28 poster with the QR code is in the repository with its layout and render script.
- After October 31 the page switches to a thank-you on its own.

Also in this release, on the donate page:

- Campaign cards show a picture only when one has been set for the campaign. Cards without one no longer borrow a generic club photo, and every card now shows the campaign's name at the top.
- Cards in the same row are the same height, with the Give button at the bottom of each.

#### Permissions

None. The page is public.

#### New Routes

- `GET /white-cane` — the campaign page
- `GET /whitecane` — forwards to the page with tracking tags
